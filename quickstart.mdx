This guide takes you from an empty workspace to a hiring decision backed by your team's evaluations.

<Note>
  Don't have an account yet? Start with [Sign up and create your company](/get-started/sign-up).
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a job">
    Go to **Hiring → Jobs** and click **Create job**. Enter the title, location, employment type, and salary, then click **Create JD for me** to let Enfue draft the description. Add the required skills and click **Save**.

    See [Creating and editing a job](/hiring/jobs/create-a-job).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish it">
    Choose **Publish on Career site** so candidates can find and apply for the job. Then open the job's **Integrations** tab to share it on LinkedIn or Facebook, or to connect it to job boards like VietnamWorks. ITviec and TopCV need your account login for each, entered once in **Settings → Applicant Source Management**. See [Job platforms](/integrations/job-platforms#itviec-and-topcv-connect-your-account).

    See [Share a job and receive applicants](/hiring/jobs/job-integrations).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add applicants you already have">
    Click **Add applicants** to add candidates manually, from a PDF CV, or from LinkedIn. If you're moving from another system, use the job's **Data Migration** to import resume PDFs, a candidate spreadsheet, or a ZIP of CVs and evaluations in one go.

    See [Adding applicants](/hiring/applicants/add-applicants) and [Import applicants into a job](/hiring/jobs/data-migration).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review applicants">
    Open the job's **Applicants** tab. Each applicant has an AI match score, so start with the highest. Click **Preview** to read their CV and AI Review, and step through applicants with **Previous** and **Next**.

    See [The applicant profile](/hiring/applicants/applicant-profile).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move them through your pipeline">
    Open **Hiring → Pipeline** and drag applicants between stages. Hover over a card for quick actions such as scheduling an interview, evaluating, or disqualifying.

    See [Pipeline](/hiring/pipeline).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule an interview">
    Click the calendar icon on the applicant's card, or **Schedule appointment** on the **Interviews** page. Pick the date and time, add at least one interviewer, and choose in-person or video call. The interviewers you add are asked to evaluate the applicant afterwards.

    See [Interviews](/hiring/interviews#schedule-an-interview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collect your team's evaluations">
    To ask anyone else for their opinion, click **Request Evaluations** on the **Evaluations** page. Send the request by email, or generate a link to share in Zalo, Slack, or Teams. Each interviewer rates the applicant, picks **Recommend** or **Do not Recommend**, and writes a note.

    See [Evaluations](/hiring/evaluations).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your decision">
    Check the **Evaluations** page for anything still **In Progress** or **Pending**, then compare the recommendations on the applicant's profile and Pipeline card. Move the applicant to your next stage, or disqualify them.
  </Step>
</Steps>
