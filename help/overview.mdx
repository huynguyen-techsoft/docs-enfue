## Talk to us

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Message us" icon="message">
    Chat with our support team. Open **Message us** from the **Help** menu in Enfue.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Contact us" icon="paper-plane" href="https://enfue.com/contact-us/">
    Get in touch with the Enfue team.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

You can also email [support@enfue.com](mailto:support@enfue.com).

<Tip>
  Is something not loading? Check [System Status](https://status.enfue.com) first to see whether Enfue is having an outage.
</Tip>

## Help us improve Enfue

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Report an issue" icon="bug" href="https://app.enfue.com/job/enfuehelpdesk/issuereport?view=public">
    Something not working as it should? Tell us what happened.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Suggest an improvement" icon="lightbulb" href="https://app.enfue.com/job/enfuehelpdesk/featuresuggestion?view=public">

  </Card>

  When you report an issue, include what you were trying to do, what happened instead, and a screenshot if you can. It helps us fix it much faster.
</Tip>
