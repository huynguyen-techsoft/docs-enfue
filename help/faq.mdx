<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is there an Enfue mobile app?">
    Not yet, but Enfue works well in your phone's browser at [app.enfue.com](https://app.enfue.com), with a layout made for small screens. See [Enfue on mobile](/mobile/overview).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I add, remove, or reorder hiring stages?">
    No. Every workspace has exactly five stages. You can rename them to match how you hire in [Applicant stages](/settings/applications/applicant-stages).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the match score?">
    A score out of 100 from Enfue's AI showing how well an applicant fits the job, with a factor-by-factor breakdown. See [AI Review](/hiring/applicants/applicant-profile#ai-review).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do interviewers need an Enfue admin seat?">
    No. People who only interview or evaluate candidates don't use an admin seat. Add them as [interviewers](/settings/users/interviewers), and send them evaluation requests by email or link.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="An interviewer doesn't read email. How do I get their evaluation?">
    Request the evaluation **via link** and share it in Zalo, Slack, or Teams. Links expire after 3 days by default. See [Request an evaluation](/hiring/evaluations#request-an-evaluation).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What does Pending mean on an evaluation?">
    The deadline has passed and the interviewer hasn't submitted yet. **In Progress** means there's still time, and **Completed** means it's been submitted. See [Evaluation statuses](/hiring/evaluations#evaluation-statuses).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="When should I use the Inbox and when Messenger?">
    Use the [Inbox](/hiring/inbox) for formal emails, attachments, templates, and candidates without an Enfue account. Use [Messenger](/hiring/messenger) for quick questions and reminders to candidates who have an Enfue account.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I move my candidates from another system?">
    Use [Data migration](/settings/general/data-migration) to import resume PDFs, a candidate spreadsheet, or a ZIP of CVs and evaluations.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when I downgrade my plan?">
    The downgrade takes effect at the end of your current billing cycle. Check first that your admins and stored applicants fit the smaller plan. See [Subscription](/payments/subscription).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Who can change settings and billing?">
    **Super Admins** have full control, including settings, billing, and admin management. **Admins** manage jobs, applicants, interviews, and reports. See [Admins](/settings/users/admins).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

More questions are answered on the [Enfue website FAQ](https://enfue.com/faq). Still stuck? [Get help](/help/overview).
