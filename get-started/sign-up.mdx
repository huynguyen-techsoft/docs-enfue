Getting started with Enfue takes three steps: create your account, create your company profile, and invite your team.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [app.enfue.com](https://app.enfue.com) and sign up in whichever way suits you:

    - **Email:** sign up with your work email address and a password.
    - **Google:** click **Sign up with Google** and choose your Google account.
    - **LinkedIn:** click **Sign up with LinkedIn** and sign in to LinkedIn. You can also fill in your Enfue profile from LinkedIn.

    Already have an account? Sign in the same way you signed up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your company profile">
    Create a new company profile for your business, with your company's name and details. Whoever creates the company becomes its first admin, with full control of the company, including settings, billing, and inviting other admins.

    You can complete your company's public page later. See [Career page](/workplace/career-page).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your colleagues">
    Invite your HR team to join your company as admins. They'll get an email invitation, and can create their account with their email address, Google, or LinkedIn. See [Admins](/settings/users/admins#add-an-admin).

    People who only interview or evaluate candidates don't need an admin account. Add them as [interviewers](/settings/users/interviewers) instead.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use your work email address, or the Google or LinkedIn account linked to it, so your colleagues can find and invite you easily.
</Tip>
