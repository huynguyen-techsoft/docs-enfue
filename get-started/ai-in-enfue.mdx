## Where AI helps along the way

| Step | What the AI does | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Create a job** | Drafts the job description. | [Create and edit a job](/hiring/jobs/create-a-job) |
| **Find candidates** | Searches your talent pools for good matches. | [Find matches with AI](/hiring/jobs/talent-discovery) |
| **Screen applicants** | Scores each applicant and explains the score. | [Understand the AI match score](/hiring/applicants/ai-review) |
| **Interview** | Asks your questions in a spoken interview, follows up, and scores the answers. | [AI interview questions](/settings/applications/ai-interview-questions) |
| **Evaluate** | Shows interviewers the match score next to the CV. | [Request and give evaluations](/hiring/evaluations) |

## The AI services behind Enfue

- [OpenAI](/integrations/openai) and [Claude](/integrations/claude) power scoring, summaries, job descriptions, and Fil.
- [ElevenLabs](/integrations/elevenlabs) gives AI interviews their natural-sounding voice.

<Note>
  AI helps you work faster, but your team makes the hiring decisions. Review AI scores and summaries alongside the CV and your interviewers' evaluations, and never disqualify someone on the score alone.
</Note>
