New to Enfue? Here's how everything fits together, in five minutes.

## The building blocks

| | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Company** | Your workspace in Enfue. It has a public [career page](/workplace/career-page), your team, and your settings. See [Sign up](/get-started/sign-up). |
| **Job** | A role you're hiring for, either public (on your career page) or internal. See [Jobs](/hiring/jobs/overview). |
| **Applicant** | A person who applied for a job or whom your team added. Each applicant has one [profile](/hiring/applicants/applicant-profile) with their CV, AI match score, evaluations, emails, and history. |
| **Stage** | A step in your hiring process. Every company has exactly five, such as **Applied** and **Hired**, which you can rename. See [Applicant stages](/settings/applications/applicant-stages). |
| **Evaluation** | An interviewer's ratings, recommendation, and note about an applicant. See [Evaluations](/hiring/evaluations). |
| **Talent pool** | A group of promising people you keep for future roles, across all your jobs. See [Talent Pools](/hiring/talent-pools). |

## The people on your team

- **Super Admins** control everything, including settings and billing.
- **Admins** run hiring day to day: jobs, applicants, interviews, and reports, for all jobs or only some.
- **Interviewers** interview and evaluate candidates. They don't need an admin seat.

See [Admins](/settings/users/admins) and [Interviewers](/settings/users/interviewers).

## How applicants flow through Enfue

<Steps>
  <Step title="They arrive">
    From your career page, LinkedIn, job boards, email, your website, imports, or Enfue's talent partners. Each gets an AI match score.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You review them">
    In [Tasks](/hiring/tasks) and [Applicants](/hiring/applicants/overview), sorted by match score.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They move through your stages">
    On the [Pipeline](/hiring/pipeline), with interviews and evaluations along the way.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You hire, or keep them for later">
    Hired applicants appear on the [Hired](/hiring/hired) page. Others are disqualified with a reason, or added to a talent pool.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Where to find things

The left menu in Enfue follows the same order as these docs' [Guides](/hiring/home): your day (Home, Tasks, Calendar), jobs, sourcing, applicants, the hiring process, communication, and insights.

## Where AI helps

Enfue's AI works alongside your team at every step:

- **Create JD for me** drafts a job description for you. See [Create and edit a job](/hiring/jobs/create-a-job).
- **AI Review** scores every applicant out of 100 against the job. See [Understand the AI match score](/hiring/applicants/ai-review).
- **Fil**, your AI assistant, finds people in your talent pools who fit a new role. See [Talent Discovery](/hiring/jobs/talent-discovery).
- **AI interviews** ask your questions and score the answers. See [AI interview questions](/settings/applications/ai-interview-questions).
