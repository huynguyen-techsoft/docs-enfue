This page follows one job from start to finish. Use it as a map: each step links to the guide with the details.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the job">
    Enter the title, location, and salary, let AI draft the description with **Create JD for me**, and add the skills you need. See [Create and edit a job](/hiring/jobs/create-a-job).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Advertise it">
    Publish it on your career page, share it on LinkedIn and Facebook, connect it to job boards like VietnamWorks and ITviec, and embed it on your website. See [Advertise a job](/hiring/jobs/job-integrations).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring in more candidates">
    Add people you already know, import your existing data, search your talent pools with AI, or get candidates from Enfue's talent partners. See [Adding applicants](/hiring/applicants/add-applicants), [Find matches with AI](/hiring/jobs/talent-discovery), and [Get candidates from talent partners](/hiring/jobs/talentspike).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Screen applicants">
    Work through [Tasks](/hiring/tasks), starting with the highest [AI match scores](/hiring/applicants/ai-review). Read each CV, **Proceed** the promising ones, and [disqualify](/hiring/disqualify) the rest with a reason.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Interview">
    Schedule interviews in person or by video call, and book rooms and interviewers. See [Schedule interviews](/hiring/interviews).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collect evaluations">
    Ask your interviewers for their ratings and recommendation, by email or with a link they can open in Zalo, Slack, or Teams. See [Request and give evaluations](/hiring/evaluations).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep candidates informed">
    Email them from the shared [Inbox](/hiring/inbox) using templates, or send a quick message in [Messenger](/hiring/messenger).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide and make an offer">
    Compare evaluations, move your chosen candidate to your offer stage on the [Pipeline](/hiring/pipeline), and send the offer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hire">
    Move them to **Hired**. They appear on the Hired page, where you can track their onboarding. See [Track your hires](/hiring/hired).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep the runners-up">
    Add strong candidates who weren't chosen to a [talent pool](/hiring/talent-pools) for your next role.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Check how the job went in its [report](/hiring/jobs/job-report): how many applied, from which channels, and how long hiring took.
</Tip>
