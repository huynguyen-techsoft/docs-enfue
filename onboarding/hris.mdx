Hiring doesn't stop at the offer. Enfue's HRIS helps you keep track of the people you've hired and look after your team once they've joined.

<Note>
  **Time needed:** about 15 minutes. **Who:** HR managers.
</Note>

## What to do

<Steps>
  <Step title="See your hires">
    Open the **Hired** page to see everyone you've hired, their start date, and their onboarding state, with reports on time to hire and hiring sources. See [Hired](/hiring/hired).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep your people records up to date">
    Use HRIS as your team's database: track hired team members and their work anniversaries, and update many records at once with bulk editing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  A step-by-step guide to HRIS is coming soon. In the meantime, email [support@enfue.com](mailto:support@enfue.com) with any questions.
</Note>

## You're done when

- You've checked the onboarding state of your recent hires on the Hired page.
