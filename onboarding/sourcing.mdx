You probably already have candidates in spreadsheets, inboxes, or another system. Bring them into Enfue so everything is in one place, then organize them for future roles.

<Note>
  **Time needed:** 30 minutes to a few hours, depending on how much data you're moving. **Who:** recruiters, or whoever manages your candidate data.
</Note>

## What to do

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add applicants in different ways">
    Add candidates one at a time: manually, from a PDF CV, from a LinkedIn profile, or with the Chrome extension. See [Adding applicants](/hiring/applicants/add-applicants) and [Chrome extension](/integrations/chrome-extension).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Migrate your existing data">
    Import resume PDFs, a candidate spreadsheet, or a ZIP of CVs and evaluations from your old system. See [Data migration](/settings/general/data-migration).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take bulk actions on many applicants">
    Select several applicants to change their stage, email them, or add them to a talent pool at once. See [Bulk actions](/hiring/applicants/bulk-actions).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create and edit talent pools">
    Group promising people, such as runners-up or graduates from a campus event, so you can come back to them later. See [Talent Pools](/hiring/talent-pools).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## You're done when

- Your existing candidates are in Enfue.
- You've created at least one talent pool.

<Tip>
  After a big import, check each job's [Duplicates](/hiring/jobs/duplicates) tab for people who were already in Enfue, and use [tags](/hiring/applicants/tags) to label candidates for quick filtering.
</Tip>
