Welcome to Enfue. This checklist takes you through everything a new team needs, in a sensible order: from seeing your hiring data and setting up your brand, through posting jobs and finding candidates, to running your hiring process and configuring your workspace.

Each step has its own page in the left menu, with what to do, how long it takes, and how you'll know you're done.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reporting" icon="chart-line">
    View and download your hiring reports. [Go to step 1 →](/onboarding/reporting)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Branding" icon="building">
    Set up your career page, and schedule events. [Go to step 2 →](/onboarding/branding)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Job posting" icon="briefcase">
    Create a job post, link it to LinkedIn Jobs, and add a job form to your website. [Go to step 3 →](/onboarding/job-posting)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sourcing" icon="magnifying-glass">
    Add applicants in different ways, migrate your data, take bulk actions, and create talent pools. [Go to step 4 →](/onboarding/sourcing)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Processing" icon="user-group">
    Use the Kanban board, evaluations, appointments, emails, and notes. [Go to step 5 →](/onboarding/processing)
  </Step>
  <Step title="HRIS" icon="id-card">
    Track your new hires and team members. [Go to step 6 →](/onboarding/hris)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Settings" icon="gear">
    Edit your hiring stages and email templates, add admins and interviewers, and check your plan. [Go to step 7 →](/onboarding/settings)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You don't have to finish every step before you start hiring. Many teams post their first job after step 3 and come back to the rest as they go.
</Tip>

Need a hand? Email [support@enfue.com](mailto:support@enfue.com).
