Welcome to Enfue. This checklist takes you through everything a new team needs, in a sensible order: from seeing your hiring data and setting up your brand, through posting jobs and finding candidates, to running your whole hiring process and configuring your workspace.

Each step links to a guide. Work through them at your own pace, and tick them off as you go.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reporting">
    - [View and download reports](/hiring/reports)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Branding">
    - [Edit your career page](/workplace/career-page)
    - Schedule an event (guide coming soon)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Job posting">
    - [Create a job post](/hiring/jobs/create-a-job)
    - [Link Enfue jobs to LinkedIn Jobs](/hiring/jobs/job-integrations#receive-applicants-from-linkedin-jobs)
    - [Add a job embedded form to your website](/hiring/jobs/job-integrations#embed-the-job-form-on-your-website)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sourcing">
    - [Add applicants in multiple ways](/hiring/applicants/add-applicants)
    - [Migrate your data into Enfue](/settings/general/data-migration)
    - [Use bulk actions on multiple applicants](/hiring/applicants/bulk-actions)
    - [Create and edit talent pools](/hiring/talent-pools)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Processing">
    | Area | Action |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Kanban** | [Use the Kanban board](/hiring/pipeline) |
    | **Evaluations** | [Add and request evaluations](/hiring/evaluations) |
    | **Appointments** | [Set up and manage appointments](/hiring/interviews), and see them all in the [Calendar](/hiring/calendar) |
    | **Emails** | [Send and track emails with applicants](/hiring/inbox), using your [email templates](/settings/applications/email-templates) |
    | **Notes** | [Use Notes](/hiring/notes) |
  </Step>
  <Step title="HRIS">
    - Use HRIS (guide coming soon)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Settings">
    - [Edit your hiring stages](/settings/applications/applicant-stages)
    - [Edit your email templates](/settings/applications/email-templates)
    - [Add admins](/settings/users/admins)
    - [Add interviewers](/settings/users/interviewers)
    - [Check your current subscription plan](/payments/subscription)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You don't have to finish every step before you start hiring. Many teams post their first job after step 3 and come back to the rest as they go.
</Tip>

Need a hand? Email [support@enfue.com](mailto:support@enfue.com).
