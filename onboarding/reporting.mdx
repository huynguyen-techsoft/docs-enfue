Start with the numbers. Enfue builds your hiring reports automatically, so as soon as applicants arrive, you can see how hiring is going.

<Note>
  **Time needed:** about 10 minutes. **Who:** anyone who reports on hiring, such as HR managers and team leaders.
</Note>

## What to do

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Reports">
    Open **Reports** from the main menu and look at **Hiring Health at a Glance**: new applicants, interviews, actions, and hires in the last 7 days. See [Reports](/hiring/reports).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a period and jobs">
    Pick the dates you want to report on, and filter by job if you only need some of them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the section for your role">
    **Pipeline Detail** is for hiring managers, **Recruiter Activity** for recruiters, and **Team Performance** for team leaders.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download or share a report">
    Click **Share Report** to create a PDF link, or use the arrow next to it to **Download PDF** or **Export CSV**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## You're done when

- You know where to find **Reports** and which section is yours.
- You've downloaded or shared your first report.

<Tip>
  Check [Home](/hiring/home) every morning for today's numbers, and Reports once a week for the bigger picture.
</Tip>
