Finally, set up Enfue to match how your company hires, and bring your whole team in.

<Note>
  **Time needed:** about 30 minutes. **Who:** a Super Admin.
</Note>

## What to do

<Steps>
  <Step title="Edit your hiring stages">
    Rename your five stages to match your process, such as **Phone Screen** or **Technical Interview**. See [Applicant stages](/settings/applications/applicant-stages).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit your email templates">
    Write templates for the emails you send most, such as an acknowledgement, an interview invitation, a job offer, and a refusal. See [Email templates](/settings/applications/email-templates).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add admins">
    Invite your HR team, and choose each person's role and which jobs they can access. See [Admins](/settings/users/admins).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add interviewers">
    Add the people who interview and evaluate candidates. They don't need an admin seat. See [Interviewers](/settings/users/interviewers).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your subscription plan">
    See your current plan, how much of it you're using, and how to upgrade. See [Subscription](/payments/subscription).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## You're done when

- Your stages and email templates match how you hire.
- Your HR team and interviewers have been invited.

<Tip>
  Explore the rest of [Settings](/settings/overview) too, such as your application form, disqualified reasons, and integrations with your calendar and video tools.
</Tip>
