Now create a job and share it everywhere your candidates look. Applications from every channel land in Enfue automatically.

<Note>
  **Time needed:** about 20 minutes per job. **Who:** recruiters and hiring managers.
</Note>

## What to do

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a job post">
    Go to **Hiring → Jobs** and click **Create job**. Enter the title, location, employment type, and salary, then click **Create JD for me** to let AI draft the description. See [Create and edit a job](/hiring/jobs/create-a-job).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish it on your career page">
    Choose **Publish on Career site** so candidates can find and apply for the job.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link it to LinkedIn Jobs">
    Use the job's Enfue application link in your LinkedIn job post, so LinkedIn applicants land in Enfue. See [LinkedIn](/integrations/linkedin).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect other job boards">
    Add the job's email address to VietnamWorks and other boards. For **ITviec** and **TopCV**, enter your account login for each in **Settings → Applicant Source Management** instead, or their applicants won't reach Enfue. See [Job platforms](/integrations/job-platforms).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the job form to your website">
    Copy the job's embedded form onto your own site, such as WordPress or Wix. See [Embed the job form on your website](/hiring/jobs/job-integrations#embed-the-job-form-on-your-website).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## You're done when

- Your first job is published on your career page.
- It's linked to LinkedIn Jobs and at least one other job board or your website.

<Tip>
  Track which channel brings the best candidates with each job's [report](/hiring/jobs/job-report).
</Tip>
