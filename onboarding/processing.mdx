This is where hiring happens day to day. Learn the five tools your team uses to move applicants from first look to offer.

<Note>
  **Time needed:** about 45 minutes to try each tool once. **Who:** everyone on your hiring team.
</Note>

## What to do

<Steps>
  <Step title="Use the Kanban board">
    See every applicant in a column for their stage, and drag them forward as they progress. See [Pipeline](/hiring/pipeline).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add and request evaluations">
    Evaluate applicants yourself, and ask interviewers for their ratings and recommendation by email or with a link you share in Zalo, Slack, or Teams. See [Evaluations](/hiring/evaluations).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up and manage appointments">
    Schedule interviews, book rooms or video calls, and see everything in the calendar. See [Interviews](/hiring/interviews) and [Calendar](/hiring/calendar).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send and track emails with applicants">
    Email applicants from your team's shared Inbox, using templates for the messages you send most. See [Inbox](/hiring/inbox) and [Email templates](/settings/applications/email-templates).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use Notes">
    Write meeting minutes and interview debriefs together, in real time. See [Notes](/hiring/notes).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## You're done when

- You've moved an applicant on the Kanban board and scheduled an interview.
- You've requested an evaluation and sent an email from the Inbox.

<Tip>
  Make [Tasks](/hiring/tasks) your daily to-do list: it shows the applicants waiting for you to decide on them.
</Tip>
