Candidates check out your company before they apply. Your career page on Enfue is where they learn who you are, what it's like to work with you, and which jobs are open.

<Note>
  **Time needed:** about 30 minutes. **Who:** a Super Admin or whoever looks after your employer brand.
</Note>

## What to do

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fill in your company header">
    Add your logo, tagline, industry, location, and links to your website, LinkedIn, and Facebook. See [Career page](/workplace/career-page#the-page-header).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell your story on the About tab">
    Fill in **About Us**, **Services**, **Benefits**, and **Activities**, with real photos of your team. See [About](/workplace/career-page#about).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your contact details and offices">
    Enter your website, email, phone number, headquarters, and branches. See [Contact](/settings/general/contact).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your career page address">
    Pick a short, recognizable address, such as `app.enfue.com/acmetech`. See [Custom URL](/settings/general/custom-url).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your first post">
    Share some news or a team story on the **Posts** tab. See [Posts](/workplace/career-page#posts).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule an event (optional)">
    Plan events such as job fairs or campus visits. A step-by-step guide is coming soon.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## You're done when

- Every tab of your career page has content, with no empty sections.
- You've chosen your custom URL and shared it with your team.

<Tip>
  Match Enfue's colors to your brand too. See [Appearance](/settings/general/appearance).
</Tip>
