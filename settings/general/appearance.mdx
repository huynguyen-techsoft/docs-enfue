---

**Appearance** lets you give Enfue your company's colors, so it feels like your own tool.

Open **Settings → Appearance**.

| Setting | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| **Background color** | The background of the top header and the left-side menu, for example `#353873`. |
| **Text color** | The text color, applied to all text, for example `#FCFCFD`. |
| **Set to default** | Turn it on to go back to Enfue's standard colors. |

Click a color to pick a new one, or type its hex code. The **Preview** shows how the header and menu will look with your colors.

<Tip>
  Choose a text color that stands out clearly against your background color, such as white text on a dark background. If the menu is hard to read, your team will feel it every day.
</Tip>
