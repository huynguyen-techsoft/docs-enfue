Your **Custom URL** is the web address of your company's [career page](/workplace/career-page) on Enfue, for example `https://app.enfue.com/acmetech`. Pick something short and recognizable, usually your company name, so it's easy to share with candidates.

## Change your custom URL

1. Open **Settings → Custom URL**.
2. Type the part after `https://app.enfue.com/`. Use at least 5 characters: letters and numbers only, with no spaces or symbols.
3. Click **Save**.

<Warning>
  Changing your custom URL changes the address of your career page. Links you've already shared, for example in job posts, email signatures, or social media, may stop working. Choose your URL early and keep it.
</Warning>
