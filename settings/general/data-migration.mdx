**Data Migration** brings your existing candidates into Enfue in bulk, for example when you move from another recruitment system or a set of spreadsheets. You can also import into a single job from that job's [Data Migration tab](/hiring/jobs/data-migration).

Open **Settings → Data Migration** and choose one of three import types.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Resume PDFs">
    Import CVs as PDF files, one by one or bundled in a `.zip` file.

    1. Choose **Resume PDFs (.zip/.pdf)**.
    2. Under **Select job to import the data**, pick the job these candidates applied for.
    3. Drag and drop your PDF or ZIP files, or click **browse**.
    4. Check your files in the panel on the right. Use **All**, **PDF**, and **ZIP** to filter them, and **Search uploaded files** to find one.
    5. Click **Upload**.

    Enfue reads each CV and creates an applicant from it.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Candidate Spreadsheet">
    Import a list of candidates from a `.csv`, `.xls`, or `.xlsx` file.

    1. Choose **Candidate Spreadsheet (.csv/.xls/.xlsx)**.
    2. Not sure how to lay out your file? Click **Download an example template**, and copy your candidates into it.
    3. Drag and drop your file, or click **browse**.
    4. Tick **Invite them to join Enfue and chat with you directly** if you'd like to invite these candidates to create an Enfue account. Once they join, you can message them in [Messenger](/hiring/messenger).
    5. Click **Upload**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="CVs & Evaluations">
    Bring over candidates together with your team's evaluations of them, for example from your previous system.

    1. Choose **CVs & Evaluations (.zip)**.
    2. Under **Select job to import the data**, pick the job.
    3. Prepare one `.zip` file laid out as shown below.
    4. Drag and drop the ZIP file, or click **browse**.
    5. Click **Upload**.

    The ZIP file must contain a folder for each applicant, and each applicant's folder must contain their **CV as a PDF** and an **evaluation as a Word file**:

    ```text
    Front-End Engineer.zip
    └── Front-End Engineer/
        ├── 1 - Linh Nguyen/
        │   ├── Evaluation note.docx
        │   └── Resume_Linh-Nguyen_FE.pdf
        ├── 2 - Minh Tran/
        │   ├── Evaluation note.docx
        │   └── Resume_Minh-Tran_FE.pdf
        └── 3 - Mai Pham/
            ├── Evaluation note.docx
            └── Resume_Mai-Pham_FE.pdf
    ```
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Tip>
  After a large import, check the job's [Duplicates](/hiring/jobs/duplicates) tab for candidates who were already in Enfue.
</Tip>
