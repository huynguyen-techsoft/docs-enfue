**Contact** holds your company's contact details and office locations. Candidates see them on the **Contact** tab of your [career page](/workplace/career-page#contact).

Open **Settings → Contact**. It has two sections: **Contact Info** and **Locations**. Click the **pencil** next to a section to edit it.

## Contact Info

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Website** | Required. Your company website. |
| **Email** | Where candidates can email you. |
| **Phone number** | Required. |
| **LinkedIn** | Your company's LinkedIn page. |
| **Facebook** | Your company's Facebook page. |

Your LinkedIn and Facebook links also appear as icons in the header of your career page.

Click **Save**, or **Cancel** to discard your changes.

## Locations

1. Enter your **Headquarters** address. This is required.
2. Click **Add new branch** to add another office, and enter its address. Click the **bin** next to a branch to remove it.
3. Click **Save**.

Your offices appear on a map on your career page, so candidates can see where they'd be working.

<Tip>
  Use full addresses, including the city and country. The map can then place every office correctly.
</Tip>
