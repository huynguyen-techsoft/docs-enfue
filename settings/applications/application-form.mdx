The application form is what candidates fill in when they apply for one of your jobs, on your [career page](/workplace/career-page) or through a [form embedded on your website](/hiring/jobs/job-integrations#embed-the-job-form-on-your-website). Use **Application Form** to decide what's required and how the form is worded.

Open **Settings → Application Form**. It has two tabs: **Fields** and **Language labels**.

## Fields

Turn each option on or off with its switch. Changes apply to the public application form immediately.

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Phone number required** | Applicants must give a phone number. |
| **CV section visible** | Shows the CV upload section. Turn it off for LinkedIn-only application flows. |
| **CV required** | Applicants must upload a CV. |
| **Video CV section visible** | Shows the video CV upload section. |
| **Video CV required** | Applicants must upload a video CV. |
| **Send confirmation email to applicant** | Emails applicants when their application is submitted. Turn it off if you send confirmations from your own [email template](/settings/applications/email-templates). |

To ask applicants your own questions, such as their years of experience, add a [custom field for applicants](/settings/applications/custom-fields-for-applicants) and tick **Show in the application form**.

<Tip>
  Making a video CV optional rather than required is a good middle ground: applicants who want to stand out can add one, and nobody is put off applying.
</Tip>

## Language labels

Every piece of text applicants see on your job pages and application form can be changed here, so you can show the form in your preferred language, such as Vietnamese.

The labels are grouped by where they appear, for example:

- **Header**: **Apply this job**, **Apply**, **Reapply**, **Location:**, **Skills**.
- **About the job**: **About job**, **Company information**, **Industry**, **Company size**, **Website**.
- The form itself: **How do you know this job**, **Additional Information**, **Cancel**, **Apply**.
- **CV upload**: **Upload CV**, **Use .doc, .docx or .pdf files, max size of 5MB**, **Video Intro**, **Upload video CV**.

Each label shows its original text on the left. Type your own wording in the box on the right.

<Note>
  Some labels include a placeholder, such as **Follow** {company} **to keep up with latest updates**. Keep {company} in your wording where your company name should appear.
</Note>

Click **Reset to default** to go back to the original labels.
