This is where you set up the evaluation form your interviewers fill in: the criteria they rate, such as **Technical Skills** and **Communication**, and the decision buttons they choose from. The form applies to every [evaluation](/hiring/evaluations) in your workspace.

Open **Settings → Custom Fields for Evaluation**. It has two tabs: **Custom Fields** and **Settings**.

## Custom Fields

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **#** | The field's position in the form. Drag the handle (**⋮⋮**) to change the order. |
| **Name** | The criterion, for example **Technical Skills**. |
| **Created** | When it was added. |
| **Show in form for interviewer** | **Yes** if interviewers see it in the evaluation form. |
| **Type** | The kind of answer, such as **Star rating**. |
| **Action** | Click the pencil to edit the field, or the bin to delete it. |

### Add a field

1. Click **Add custom field**.
2. Enter a **Field name**.
3. Choose a **Field type**: **Text**, **Date**, **Multi select**, or **Star rating**.
4. Tick **Show in form for interviewer** so interviewers see it.
5. Click **Add**.

<Tip>
  Use **Star rating** for the criteria you want to compare across candidates, such as **Technical Skills** or **Culture Fit**, and **Multi select** for a fixed choice, such as the candidate's **Level**.
</Tip>

## Settings

The **Settings** tab controls the decision buttons interviewers see at the end of the form.

- **Neutral decision**: turn this on to add a third option for interviewers who aren't ready to recommend or reject an applicant.
- **Decision button labels**: change the text on each button. By default they're **Do not Recommend**, **Neutral**, and **Recommend**.
- **Preview**: shows how the buttons will look in the evaluation form.

Click **Save changes**, or **Reset to default** to go back to the original labels.

<Note>
  A neutral option is handy when several people interview the same candidate, but use it sparingly. If most evaluations come back neutral, they won't help you decide.
</Note>
