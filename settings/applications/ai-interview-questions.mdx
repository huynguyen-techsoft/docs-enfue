When you invite an applicant to an [AI interview](/hiring/applicants/applicant-profile#ai-interview), Enfue's AI asks them questions and scores their answers. **AI Interview Questions** is where you decide what it asks and what matters most. The same criteria and questions are used for every AI interview in your company.

Open **Settings → AI Interview Questions**.

## How it's organized

Under **Criteria & questions**, the interview is split into **criteria**, such as **Technical Proficiency**, **Problem Solving**, **Team Collaboration**, **Adaptability**, and **Motivation & Initiative**.

- Each criterion has a **weight**, for example **30%**, that sets how much it counts toward the applicant's result. Click **−** or **+** to change it.
- Each criterion has one or more **questions**, numbered **Q1**, **Q2**, and so on.
- Each question has a **Follow-ups** setting. With **AI decides**, the AI chooses whether to ask follow-up questions based on the applicant's answer.

## Create your questions

Click **Create** to start. You can write your criteria and questions from scratch, or let AI draft a starting point for you and edit it.

## Edit your questions

- Click **Add question** under a criterion to add a question to it.
- Click the **bin** next to a question to delete it, or next to a criterion's weight to delete the whole criterion.
- Change a criterion's weight with **−** and **+**.

Click **Save** when you're done. Changes apply to every new AI interview.

<Tip>
  Give the most weight to what really decides success in your roles. Keep the weights adding up to 100%, so each one reads as a clear share of the result.
</Tip>
