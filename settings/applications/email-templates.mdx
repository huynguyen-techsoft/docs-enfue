Email templates save you from writing the same emails over and over. Write an acknowledgement, invitation, or job offer once, and your team can send it from the [Inbox](/hiring/inbox) with a single click under **Use email template**. Parameters such as the applicant's name and the job title are filled in automatically for each applicant.

Open **Settings → Email Templates**. Your templates are listed on the left, for example **Acknowledgement**, **Invitation**, **Job Offer**, **Refusal**, **Follow-up**, and **Interview Schedule**.

## Create or edit a template

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a template">
    Click **New Template**, or click an existing template in the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Enter the template's name at the top, for example **Acknowledgement**. Your team sees this name when they pick a template.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the subject">
    Enter the **Subject**, for example **Application Received - Thank you for applying to Acme Tech**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the email">
    Write the message in the editor. Use the toolbar to format text, and add links, tables, images such as your logo, and your company signature.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add parameters">
    Click the { } button, in the toolbar or next to the subject, and pick a parameter to insert it. See [Parameters](#parameters).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**, or **Cancel** to discard your changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Parameters

Parameters are placeholders that Enfue replaces with real details when the email is sent. Search for one with **Search parameters**.

| Group | Parameter | Replaced with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Applicant** | **APPLICANT NAME** | The applicant's full name. |
| | **APPLICANT FIRSTNAME** | The applicant's first name. |
| | **JOB TITLE** | The job they applied for. |
| **Company** | **COMPANY NAME** | Your company's name. |
| | **RECRUITER NAME** | The name of the team member sending the email. |

For example, **Dear APPLICANT NAME,** becomes **Dear Linh Nguyen,** when sent to that applicant.

<Tip>
  Start with templates for the emails you send most: an acknowledgement when someone applies, an interview invitation, a job offer, and a polite refusal. Include your company signature in each, so every email looks the same.
</Tip>
