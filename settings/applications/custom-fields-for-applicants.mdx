Custom fields let you record information about applicants that Enfue doesn't track by default. Use them in two ways:

- **Ask applicants**: show the field on your application form, for example **Your years of relevant experience to this position** or **Rate your English proficiency in both verbal and written communication**.
- **For your team only**: keep the field off the form and fill it in yourselves, for example **Candidate ranking** or **Onboarding**.

Open **Settings → Custom Fields for Applicants**.

## Your custom fields

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | The field's name, as applicants or your team see it. |
| **Type** | The kind of answer, such as **Text** or **Multi selection**. |
| **Date created** | When the field was added. |
| **Show in form for applicant** | **Yes** if applicants see it on the application form. |
| **Action** | Click the pencil to edit the field, or the bin to delete it. |

## Add a custom field

1. Click **Add custom field**.
2. Enter a **Field name**.
3. Choose a **Field type**:
   - **Text**: a free-text answer.
   - **Date**: a date.
   - **Multi select**: one or more options from a list you set.
   - **Star rating**: a rating out of 5 stars.
4. Tick **Show in the application form** if applicants should fill it in.
5. Click **Add**.

Applicants' answers appear on the **Additional Info** tab of their [profile](/hiring/applicants/applicant-profile#profile-details), and you can show each field as a column on the [Applicants](/hiring/applicants/overview#customize-the-columns) page.

## Filter applicants by your custom fields

Every custom field you create also becomes a filter. Use it on the [Applicants](/hiring/applicants/overview#the-filter-panel) page and in [Talent Pools](/hiring/talent-pools#filter-the-pool), on its own or together with Enfue's built-in filters such as job, stage, source, and tags.

1. Click **Filters**.
2. Choose one of your custom fields.
3. Pick the answers you're looking for.

For example:

| You want to find | Filter by |
| --- | --- |
| Senior candidates for a new role | **Your Level**: Senior |
| Applicants with strong English | **Rate your English proficiency**: a high rating |
| Your team's top picks | **Candidate ranking**: your highest ranking |
| Senior applicants from LinkedIn who are still active | **Your Level**: Senior + **Source**: LinkedIn + **Active applicants** |

<Tip>
  Planning to filter on a field? Make it a **Multi select** or **Star rating** field. Fixed answers are far easier to filter than free text.
</Tip>
