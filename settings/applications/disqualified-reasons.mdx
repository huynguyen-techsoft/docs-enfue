When someone on your team disqualifies an applicant, they pick a reason. **Disqualified Reasons** is where you manage that list, so the reasons match how your company really makes decisions.

Open **Settings → Disqualified Reasons**. Each reason shows when it was created. Click the pencil to rename a reason, or the bin to delete it.

Some common reasons:

- **Did Not Pass Screening** and **Did Not Pass Interview**
- **Candidate Withdrew**, **Offer Declined**, and **Accepted Another Opportunity**
- **Not Open to This Opportunity**
- **Duplicated Applicant**
- **Move to talent pool**: for good candidates who aren't right for this job but whom you want to keep for later. See [Talent Pools](/hiring/talent-pools).

## Add a reason

Click **Add disqualified reason**, enter the reason, and save it.

<Tip>
  Your reasons feed the **Disqualified Reason Breakdown** in [Reports](/hiring/reports#recruiter-activity-for-recruiters). Specific reasons, such as **Salary expectations too high** or **English Proficiency**, tell you far more about why you're losing candidates than a general one like "Not suitable".
</Tip>
