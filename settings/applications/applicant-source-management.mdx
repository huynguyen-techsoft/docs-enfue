Every applicant in Enfue has a **source**: the channel they came from. Enfue records it automatically when someone applies through a connected channel, and you can set or change it yourself. Sources power the source charts in [Reports](/hiring/reports#recruiter-activity-for-recruiters) and on the [Hired](/hiring/hired#hiring-by-source) page, so you can see which channels really work.

## The built-in sources

| Source | Where the applicant came from |
| --- | --- |
| **Approaching** | Your team reached out to them directly. This is the default when you [add an applicant manually](/hiring/applicants/add-applicants#add-an-applicant-manually). |
| **CareerPage** | Your company's [career page on Enfue](/workplace/career-page). |
| **Event** | You met them at an event, such as a job fair or a campus visit. |
| **Facebook** | A job you shared on Facebook. |
| **ITViec** | The ITviec job board, an external job board. |
| **JobSite** | Your company's own careers website, for example through a [job form embedded on your website](/hiring/jobs/job-integrations#embed-the-job-form-on-your-website). |
| **LinkedIn** | LinkedIn, for example through your [LinkedIn Jobs application link](/hiring/jobs/job-integrations#receive-applicants-from-linkedin-jobs). |
| **Referral** | Someone referred them, such as a colleague. |
| **TopCV** | The TopCV job board, an external job board. |

<Note>
  **CareerPage** and **JobSite** are easy to mix up. **CareerPage** is your company's page on Enfue, at `app.enfue.com/<your-company>`. **JobSite** is your company's own website.
</Note>

## Where you'll see sources

- On each applicant, as an icon in the **Source** column of [Applicants](/hiring/applicants/overview) and [Tasks](/hiring/tasks), and in the [applicant header](/hiring/applicants/applicant-profile#the-applicant-header), where you can click it to change it.
- In the **Source** filter on Applicants, Tasks, and [Talent Pools](/hiring/talent-pools).
- In **Source Breakdown** in [Reports](/hiring/reports#recruiter-activity-for-recruiters) and **Hiring by Source** on the [Hired](/hiring/hired#hiring-by-source) page.

## Manage your sources

Open **Settings → Applicant Source Management** to manage the list of sources your team can choose from.

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | The source, with its icon, for example **LinkedIn** or **ITViec**. |
| **Status** | **Enabled** if your team can use it. |
| **Credential** | For job boards that connect to Enfue with your account: **Connected** once it's set up, or **Required** if it still needs your account details. A dash means no connection is needed. |
| **Created Date** | When the source was added. |
| **Action** | Click the pencil to edit the source, or the bin to delete it. |

Use **All Status** and **All Credential** to filter the list.

### Add a source

1. Click **Add Source**.
2. Pick one of the **Predefined Sources**, such as **Indeed**, **Glassdoor**, **Career Viet**, **VietnamWorks**, **JobStreet**, **YBOX**, or **Glints**. Or type your own under **Name**, for example a university or a recruitment agency.
3. Set the **Status** to **Active**.
4. Click **Upload Icon** to add an icon (optional).
5. Click **Add**.

<Tip>
  Add a source for every channel you really use, and nothing more, and keep each applicant's source accurate, especially for applicants you add yourself. Your reports can only tell you which channels work if the sources are right.
</Tip>
