**Rooms** is your list of meeting rooms. Add them here, and your team can book a room when they [schedule an in-person interview](/hiring/interviews#schedule-an-interview), so two interviews never end up in the same room at the same time.

Open **Settings → Rooms**.

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | The room's name, for example **Lotus**. |
| **Email** | The room's email address, for example `lotus@acmetech.com`. |
| **Location** | Where it is, for example **Floor 2**. |
| **Capacity** | How many people it holds. |
| **Action** | Click the pencil to edit the room, or the bin to remove it. |

## Add a room

Click **New room**, enter the room's name, email, location, and capacity, and save.

<Tip>
  Check **Capacity** before booking a panel interview. A room for 5 won't fit a candidate and five interviewers.
</Tip>
