Admins are the people on your team who use Enfue day to day: recruiters, HR managers, and team leaders. **Company Admins** is where you invite them and decide what each person can do.

Open **Settings → Admins**. The banner at the top, for example **Your plan includes 5 admin seats**, shows how many admins your subscription allows.

## Roles

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Admin** | Manage jobs, applicants, interviews, and reports. |
| **Super Admin** | Full company control, including settings, billing, and admin management. |

## Your admins

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Profile** | The admin's name and photo. |
| **Role** | **Admin** or **Super Admin**. |
| **Created at** | When they were added. |
| **Full job access** | **Yes** if they can access every job. |
| **Job access** | **All Jobs**, or the number of jobs they can access, for example **2 Jobs**. |
| **Last used** | When they last used Enfue. |
| **Email** and **Phone number** | Their contact details. |
| **Action** | Click the pencil to change their role or access, or the bin to remove them. |

## Add an admin

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start">
    Click **Add admin**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their email">
    Enter their **Email address**. You can add someone who already has an Enfue account, or invite someone new.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a role">
    Choose **Admin** or **Super Admin**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose their access (Admins only)">
    Under **Access scope**, choose **All jobs**, or **Specific jobs** and pick the jobs this admin can work on. Super Admins always have access to everything.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a message (optional)">
    Write a **Personal message** to include in the invitation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Click **Send invitation**. The note above the button shows how many seats you're using, for example **Seats used: 4 active of 5 admin seats**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  When every seat is in use, you can't add another admin. Remove an admin you no longer need, or upgrade your plan for more seats.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Give **Specific jobs** access to hiring managers who only need to see their own team's jobs. Keep **Super Admin** for the few people who manage settings and billing.
</Tip>

People who only interview or evaluate candidates don't need an admin seat. Add them as [interviewers](/settings/users/interviewers) instead.
