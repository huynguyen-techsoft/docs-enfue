**Settings** is where you set up Enfue to work the way your company hires. Most settings apply to your whole workspace, so they're usually managed by a **Super Admin**. See [roles](/settings/users/admins#roles).

Open **Settings** from the main menu. Not sure where something lives? Start with what you want to do.

## Setting up Enfue for the first time

Work through these in order and your workspace is ready for your first job. Each step takes a few minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Invite your HR team">
    Add the colleagues who'll hire with you, and choose what each one can access. See [Invite your HR team](/settings/users/admins).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your hiring stages">
    Rename the five stages to match your process, such as **Phone Screen** and **Technical Interview**. See [Name your hiring stages](/settings/applications/applicant-stages).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your email templates">
    Write your acknowledgement, interview invitation, job offer, and refusal once. See [Create email templates](/settings/applications/email-templates).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up the application form">
    Decide whether a CV, video CV, or phone number is required. See [Set up the application form](/settings/applications/application-form).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize the evaluation form">
    Choose what interviewers rate, such as **Technical Skills** and **Culture Fit**. See [Customize the evaluation form](/settings/applications/custom-fields-for-evaluation).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add interviewers and rooms">
    List the people who interview for you, and your meeting rooms. See [Add interviewers](/settings/users/interviewers) and [Add meeting rooms](/settings/users/rooms).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your tools">
    Turn on Teams or Google Meet for video interviews, and connect your calendar and Slack. See [Connected tools](/integrations/connected-tools).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Moving from another system? [Import your data](/settings/general/data-migration) after step 2, so your candidates land in the right stages.
</Tip>
