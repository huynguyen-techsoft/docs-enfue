Your **career page** is your company's home on Enfue. It's where candidates get to know you: what you do, what it's like to work with you, what you offer, and which jobs are open. Candidates can follow your company, apply for jobs, and keep up with your posts, all in one place.

Your career page has its own address, `https://app.enfue.com/<your-company>`, for example `https://app.enfue.com/acmetech`. Share it anywhere you'd like candidates to find you.

## The page header

At the top of the page, candidates see:

- Your **logo** and **company name**.
- Your **industry** and **tagline**, for example **Information Technology | Building better software since 2010.**
- Your **location**, and links to your **website**, **LinkedIn**, and **Facebook**.

Click **Edit** at the top right to change these details, or the pencil on the logo to change your logo. Click the **share** icon next to it to share your career page.

Below the header, the page has seven tabs: **About**, **Posts**, **Jobs**, **Internship**, **Followers**, **Invites**, and **Contact**.

<Tip>
  Each section has its own **pencil** icon. Click it to edit just that section.
</Tip>

## About

The **About** tab tells your company's story. It has four sections:

| Section | What to put there |
| --- | --- |
| **About Us** | Who you are, your mission, and your vision. Below it, cards show when you were **Founded**, your **Company size**, and your **Headquarters**. |
| **Services** | What your company does, for example as a short list. |
| **Benefits** | Why people should work for you. Add an introduction and a photo, then group your benefits under headings such as **Compensation & Perks**, **Work-Life Balance**, and **Growth & Development**. Candidates click a heading to see the details. |
| **Activities** | Your team culture, with a description and a gallery of photos from events and team activities. |

<Tip>
  Candidates read **Benefits** and **Activities** closely. Real photos of your team and specific perks, such as "flexible working hours" or "full health insurance from your first day," are far more convincing than general claims.
</Tip>

## Posts

Use **Posts** to share news with candidates and followers, such as success stories, events, and hiring campaigns.

1. Click **What's on your mind?** and write your post.
2. Click **Media** to add photos or videos.
3. Publish the post.

Each post shows your company name, tagline, and when it was posted. Click **⋯** on a post to manage it.

<Tip>
  Link to your open roles from your posts. For example, `https://app.enfue.com/acmetech?tab=Internship` opens your career page straight on the **Internship** tab.
</Tip>

## Jobs and Internship

The **Jobs** tab lists your open jobs, and the **Internship** tab lists your open internships: every job with the employment type **Internship** appears there, and all other jobs appear under **Jobs**. The count at the top, for example **4 positions available**, shows how many are open. A job appears here when you choose **Publish on Career site** for it. See [Creating and editing a job](/hiring/jobs/create-a-job).

When a candidate clicks a job, its details open on the right:

- The job title, when it was posted, the employment type, and the location.
- **Location**, **Experience**, and **Salary**.
- Your company, with a button to follow it.
- **About job**: the industry, employment type, workplace address, and the full job description.

Candidates click **Apply** to apply, or the **bookmark** icon to save the job for later. **Open job in a new window** opens the job on its own page, which is handy for sharing a link to one job.

## Followers

**Followers** lists everyone who follows your company on Enfue, with the total at the top. These are people who are interested in working with you, so they're a ready audience every time you hire.

Find followers with **Keywords**, **Location**, and **Industry**.

## Invites

Use **Invites** to invite candidates to follow your company, so they hear about your news and jobs:

- **Invite via email**: enter an email address and click **Send invite**.
- **Share via link**: copy your invitation link, for example `https://app.enfue.com/invitation/acmetech`, and share it anywhere.
- **Share on social**: post the link on **LinkedIn**, **Facebook**, or **Threads**.

## Contact

The **Contact** tab shows how candidates can reach you:

- **Contact Info**: your **Website**, **Email**, and **Phone**.
- **Locations**: your **Headquarters** and any **Branch** offices, with their addresses on a map.

Click the pencil next to a section to update it.

## Tips for a great career page

- **Fill in every tab.** An empty section makes candidates wonder what's missing.
- **Post regularly.** A recent post shows candidates you're active and growing.
- **Grow your followers.** Share your invitation link at job fairs, on campus, and in your email signature, so more people hear about your next job.
- **Keep your contact details current**, especially your email address and office locations.
