## How hiring works in Enfue

Every job follows the same path. You [create a job](/hiring/jobs/create-a-job) and publish it, and applicants arrive from your career site, job boards, LinkedIn, email, or your own imports. Enfue gives each applicant an [AI match score](/hiring/applicants/applicant-profile#ai-review) so you know who to look at first. You move applicants through the stages in your [pipeline](/hiring/pipeline), [schedule interviews](/hiring/interviews), and ask your interviewers to [evaluate](/hiring/evaluations) each candidate. Their ratings and recommendations show up next to every applicant, so the hiring decision is based on what your whole team thinks.
