<Warning>
  Enfue doesn't integrate with Zoom. You can't create Zoom meetings or sync Zoom calls from Enfue.
</Warning>

For video interviews, connect one of the meeting tools Enfue supports. Interviews you schedule as **Video call** then get a meeting link automatically:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Microsoft Teams" icon="microsoft" href="/integrations/microsoft-teams">
    Teams meetings, calendar invitations, and emails through Microsoft.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Google Meet" icon="video" href="/integrations/google-meet">
    Meet links, calendar invitations, and emails through Google.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Would a Zoom integration help your team? Let us know through [Suggest a Feature](https://app.enfue.com/job/enfuehelpdesk/featuresuggestion?view=public).
