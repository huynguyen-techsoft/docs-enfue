Enfue uses **ElevenLabs**, a voice AI service, to run online AI interviews. It gives the AI interviewer a natural-sounding voice, so candidates can have a spoken conversation instead of filling in a form. There's nothing to set up: it's built into Enfue's AI interviews.

## How it's used

- **The AI interviewer speaks** your interview questions to the candidate.
- **Candidates answer out loud**, and the AI can ask follow-up questions based on what they say.
- Enfue's AI then scores the answers against your criteria, so you can review the results.

## Set up your AI interviews

- Choose the criteria, weights, and questions in [AI interview questions](/settings/applications/ai-interview-questions).
- Invite an applicant from the **AI Interview** section of their [profile](/hiring/applicants/applicant-profile#ai-interview).

<Tip>
  Tell candidates in your invitation that the interview is spoken, so they can find a quiet place with a working microphone.
</Tip>

Enfue's other AI features use models from [OpenAI](/integrations/openai) and [Claude](/integrations/claude).
