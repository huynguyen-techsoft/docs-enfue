Connect your Google Calendar, and your meetings appear in the Enfue [Calendar](/hiring/calendar) alongside your interviews. When you book an interview, you can see straight away whether your interviewers are free.

## Connect it

1. Open **Settings → Integrations**.
2. Under **Calendars**, click **Connect** next to **Google**.
3. Sign in to your Google account and allow access.

To bring in the latest changes at any time, click **Sync** on the [Calendar](/hiring/calendar#sync-your-calendar).

## Disconnect it

In **Settings → Integrations**, click **Disconnect** next to **Google**.

For video calls in Google Meet, see [Google Meet](/integrations/google-meet).
