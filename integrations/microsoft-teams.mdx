Connect Microsoft Teams so the interviews you schedule in Enfue come with a Teams meeting, and invitations go out through Microsoft.

## What it does

- Adds a Teams video call to interviews you schedule as **Video call**. See [Schedule an interview](/hiring/interviews#schedule-an-interview).
- Sends calendar invitations and interview emails through Microsoft.

## Turn it on

1. Open **Settings → Integrations**.
2. Under **Meeting tools**, turn on **Teams Calls**.

<Tip>
  Using Outlook for your calendar too? Connect [Microsoft Calendar](/integrations/microsoft-calendar) so your Outlook meetings appear in the Enfue [Calendar](/hiring/calendar).
</Tip>

Prefer Google? Use [Google Meet](/integrations/google-meet) instead.
