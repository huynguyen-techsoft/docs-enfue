Post your jobs on the job boards you already use, and the applicants land in Enfue automatically. No copying CVs from inbox to inbox.

## Supported job boards

| Region | Job boards |
| --- | --- |
| **Vietnam** | VietnamWorks, TopCV, ITviec, TopDev, CareerViet, Vieclam24h |
| **International** | Indeed, Glassdoor, Jobstreet, Seek |

## How it works

Every job in Enfue has its own email address, for example `job+acmetech_xxxx@enfue.com`.

1. Open the job and go to its **Integrations** tab.
2. Copy the address under **Receive applicants from other job boards**.
3. When you post the job on a job board, enter this address as the place where applications should be sent.

Applications sent there are added to the job automatically. See [Receive applicants from other job boards](/hiring/jobs/job-integrations#receive-applicants-from-other-job-boards).

## Job boards that connect with your account

Some job boards, such as **ITviec** and **TopCV**, can connect to Enfue with your account on that job board. You can see and manage these connections in **Settings → Applicant Source Management**, in the **Credential** column: **Connected** once it's set up, or **Required** if it still needs your account details. See [Applicant sources](/settings/applications/applicant-source-management#manage-your-sources).

## Add more job boards as sources

To track applicants from other channels, such as **Glints** or **YBOX**, add them as sources. See [Add a source](/settings/applications/applicant-source-management#add-a-source).
