Post your jobs on the job boards you already use, and the applicants land in Enfue automatically. No copying CVs from inbox to inbox.

## Supported job boards

Job boards connect to Enfue in one of two ways. Check which one applies to yours before you post.

| Job board | How it connects |
| --- | --- |
| **ITviec**, **TopCV** | [Your job board account](#itviec-and-topcv-connect-your-account). You must enter your login for that job board in Enfue. |
| **VietnamWorks**, **TopDev**, **CareerViet**, **Vieclam24h** | [The job's Enfue email address](#all-other-job-boards-use-the-job-email). Nothing to log in to. |
| **Indeed**, **Glassdoor**, **Jobstreet**, **Seek** | [The job's Enfue email address](#all-other-job-boards-use-the-job-email). Nothing to log in to. |

## ITviec and TopCV: connect your account

ITviec and TopCV don't forward applications by email. Enfue retrieves applicants directly from your account on these job boards, so it needs your account login for each one.

<Warning>
  **No login, no applicants.** Until you enter your ITviec or TopCV account details in Enfue, applicants who apply on those job boards are **not** brought into Enfue. They stay on the job board, and you won't see them in your applicant list, pipeline, or reports.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Applicant Source Management">
    Go to **Settings → Applicant Source Management**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the Credential column">
    Find **ITviec** and **TopCV** in the list. The **Credential** column shows:

    - **Required**: Enfue doesn't have your login yet, so no applicants are being retrieved.
    - **Connected**: your account is linked, and new applicants are brought into Enfue automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your account details">
    For each job board marked **Required**, click the pencil in the **Action** column and enter the login you use for that job board's employer account. Then save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm it says Connected">
    Make sure the **Credential** column now shows **Connected**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

See [Applicant sources](/settings/applications/applicant-source-management#manage-your-sources) for more on this screen.

<Tip>
  If you change your password on ITviec or TopCV, update it in Enfue too. Otherwise the connection stops working and new applicants stop arriving.
</Tip>

## All other job boards: use the job email

Every other job board works without logging in. Every job in Enfue has its own email address, for example `job+acmetech_xxxx@enfue.com`, and the job board sends applications to it.

1. Open the job and go to its **Integrations** tab.
2. Copy the address under **Receive applicants from other job boards**.
3. When you post the job on the job board, enter this address as the place where applications should be sent.

Applications sent there are added to the job automatically. See [Receive applicants from other job boards](/hiring/jobs/job-integrations#receive-applicants-from-other-job-boards).

<Note>
  Each job has its own email address. Use the address of the job you're posting, so applicants land in the right job.
</Note>

## Add more job boards as sources

To track applicants from other channels, such as **Glints** or **YBOX**, add them as sources. See [Add a source](/settings/applications/applicant-source-management#add-a-source).
