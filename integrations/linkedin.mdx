Enfue works with LinkedIn at every step: getting your jobs seen, receiving applications, and adding candidates you find.

## Share jobs and receive applicants

- **Share a job on LinkedIn** from the job's **Integrations** tab. See [Share on social media](/hiring/jobs/job-integrations#share-on-social-media).
- **Receive applicants from LinkedIn Jobs** by using the job's Enfue application link in your LinkedIn job post. Applicants are added to the job automatically, with **LinkedIn** as their source. See [Receive applicants from LinkedIn Jobs](/hiring/jobs/job-integrations#receive-applicants-from-linkedin-jobs).

## Add candidates from LinkedIn

- **From a LinkedIn profile:** add an applicant using their LinkedIn profile URL, or a PDF of their profile. See [Adding applicants](/hiring/applicants/add-applicants).
- **While you browse:** use the [Enfue Recruiter Chrome extension](/integrations/chrome-extension).

Each applicant profile has a **LinkedIn** tab where you can add and edit their experience and education, and their LinkedIn URL can be edited from their overview.

## Sign in with LinkedIn

Anyone can sign up for and sign in to Enfue with their LinkedIn account, and candidates can fill in their Enfue profile from LinkedIn.

<Tip>
  Track how many hires come from LinkedIn in **Source Breakdown** in [Reports](/hiring/reports#recruiter-activity-for-recruiters) and **Hiring by Source** on the [Hired](/hiring/hired#hiring-by-source) page.
</Tip>
