The **Integrations** screen in **Settings** connects Enfue to the tools your team already uses. Each integration here is set up once for your whole workspace. It has four sections: **Tools**, **Career page**, **Calendars**, and **Integrations**.

## Meeting tools

Choose which meeting tools your team uses for interviews. Turn each one on or off with its switch.

| Tool | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Teams Calls** | Microsoft Teams, for calendar invitations, emails, and video calls. |
| **Google Meet** | Google Meet, for calendar invitations, emails, and video calls. |

When you [schedule an interview](/hiring/interviews#schedule-an-interview) as a video call, Enfue uses the meeting tools you've turned on here. For details, see [Microsoft Teams](/integrations/microsoft-teams) and [Google Meet](/integrations/google-meet). Zoom isn't supported yet: see [Zoom](/integrations/zoom).

## Career page

### Career Page Connect

If your company already has its own careers website, link it to Enfue:

1. Enter its address under **Career page URL**, for example `https://company-name/careers`.
2. Turn on the **Career Page Connect** switch.

When it's on, the logo and company name on your [Enfue career page](/workplace/career-page) link to your own careers website.

### Enfue Authentication

Turn on **Enfue Authentication** to require applicants to sign in to Enfue before they apply. Candidates who aren't signed in are taken straight to the sign-up page for that job.

This gives every applicant an Enfue account, so you can chat with them directly in [Messenger](/hiring/messenger).

<Note>
  Asking applicants to sign in adds a step to applying. If you're running a campaign where you want as many applications as possible, consider whether that step is worth it.
</Note>

## Calendars

Connect your **Google** or **Outlook** calendar so your meetings appear in the Enfue [Calendar](/hiring/calendar) next to your interviews.

- Click **Connect** next to a calendar and sign in to your account.
- Click **Disconnect** to stop syncing it.

Step-by-step guides: [Google Calendar](/integrations/google-calendar) and [Microsoft Calendar](/integrations/microsoft-calendar).

<Tip>
  Connect the calendar your team really uses. Then the free slots you see when booking an interview are actually free.
</Tip>

## Slack

Connect **Slack** to get all your Enfue [notifications](/hiring/notifications) in your team's Slack workspace, such as new applications, new emails, and completed evaluations. Once connected, the Slack channel Enfue posts to is shown under **Slack**, for example `enfue_acmetech_notif`. Click **Disconnect** to stop. See [Slack](/integrations/slack) for the full guide.

## Where to find the job posting integrations

Sharing a job on social media, receiving applicants from LinkedIn and other job boards, and embedding a job form on your website are set up for each job, not here. See [Share a job and receive applicants](/hiring/jobs/job-integrations).
