---

Connect Slack, and Enfue posts all your [notifications](/hiring/notifications) to a channel in your team's Slack workspace: new applications, new emails, completed evaluations, and more. Your team sees what's happening without opening Enfue.

## Connect it

1. Open **Settings → Integrations**.
2. Under **Integrations**, connect **Slack** and choose the channel to post to.

Once connected, the channel is shown under **Slack**, for example `enfue_acmetech_notif`.

## Disconnect it

In **Settings → Integrations**, click **Disconnect** next to **Slack**.

<Tip>
  Create a dedicated channel, such as `#hiring-updates`, so Enfue notifications don't get lost among other conversations.
</Tip>
