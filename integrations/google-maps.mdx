Enfue uses Google Maps so candidates can find your offices and interview venues easily. There's nothing to set up.

## Where you'll see it

- **Interview invitations:** the venue can link to its Google Maps location, so candidates can get directions in one tap. See [Schedule an interview](/hiring/interviews#schedule-an-interview).
- **Your offices:** the headquarters and branches you add in [Contact](/settings/general/contact#locations) are shown on a map on your [career page](/workplace/career-page#contact).

<Tip>
  Enter full addresses, including the city and country, so each location lands in the right place on the map.
</Tip>

When you type an address, Enfue suggests matching places. See [Google Locations](/integrations/google-locations).
