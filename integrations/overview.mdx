Enfue works with the tools your team and your candidates already use. Integrations live in a few different places in Enfue, depending on what they connect:

| Integration | Where to set it up |
| --- | --- |
| **Connected tools**: meeting tools, calendars, Slack, your careers website, and Enfue sign-in for applicants | Once for your whole team, in **Settings → Integrations**. |
| **Job posting channels**: social media, LinkedIn Jobs, other job boards, email applications, and an application form on your website | For each job, in the job's **Integrations** tab. |
| **Chrome extension** | Under **Add applicants**, in the **enfue Chrome** tab. |

## Job posting channels at a glance

Every job has its own **Integrations** tab. Applicants who come in through any of these channels are added to the job automatically, with their source recorded so you can compare channels in the [job report](/hiring/jobs/job-report).

| Channel | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Social media** | Post the job on LinkedIn, Facebook, or Threads. |
| **LinkedIn Jobs** | Use the job's Enfue application link in your LinkedIn job post. |
| **ITviec and TopCV** | Enter your account login for each in **Settings → Applicant Source Management**. See [Connect ITviec and TopCV](/integrations/job-platforms#itviec-and-topcv-connect-your-account). |
| **Other job boards** | Add the job's own email address to Indeed, Glassdoor, Jobstreet, Seek, TopDev, VietnamWorks, CareerViet, or Vieclam24h. |
| **Email** | Candidates send their application to the job's email address. |
| **Your website** | Embed the job's application form on WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Webflow, Shopify, and more. |

See [Share a job and receive applicants](/hiring/jobs/job-integrations) for step-by-step instructions.
