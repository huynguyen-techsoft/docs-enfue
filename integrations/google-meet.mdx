Connect Google Meet so the interviews you schedule in Enfue come with a Meet link, and invitations go out through Google.

## What it does

- Adds a Google Meet video call to interviews you schedule as **Video call**. See [Schedule an interview](/hiring/interviews#schedule-an-interview).
- Sends calendar invitations and interview emails through Google.

## Turn it on

1. Open **Settings → Integrations**.
2. Under **Meeting tools**, turn on **Google Meet**.

<Tip>
  Connect [Google Calendar](/integrations/google-calendar) too, so your Google meetings appear in the Enfue [Calendar](/hiring/calendar).
</Tip>

Prefer Microsoft? Use [Microsoft Teams](/integrations/microsoft-teams) instead.
