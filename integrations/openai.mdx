Enfue uses AI models from **OpenAI** to power some of its AI features. There's nothing to set up: the AI features are built into Enfue.

## AI features in Enfue

- **AI Review and match score:** how well each applicant fits a job, with a factor-by-factor breakdown. See [Understand the AI match score](/hiring/applicants/ai-review).
- **Create JD for me:** AI writes a job description from your job details. See [Create and edit a job](/hiring/jobs/create-a-job).
- **Fil, your AI assistant:** finds people in your talent pools who fit a new job. See [Talent Discovery](/hiring/jobs/talent-discovery).
- **AI interviews:** AI asks your questions and scores the answers. See [AI interview questions](/settings/applications/ai-interview-questions).

<Note>
  AI suggestions help you decide faster, but your team makes the hiring decisions. Always review AI scores and summaries alongside the CV and your interviewers' [evaluations](/hiring/evaluations).
</Note>

## Ask ChatGPT about these docs

At the top of any page in this Help Center, open the page menu and choose **Open in ChatGPT** to ask questions about that page.

Enfue also uses [Claude](/integrations/claude).
