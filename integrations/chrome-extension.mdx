**Enfue Recruiter** is an extension for Google Chrome that turns LinkedIn into a sourcing tool for Enfue. While you browse LinkedIn, you can add people to Enfue in one click, attach them to a job and talent pool, and skip copying their details by hand.

<Card title="Install Enfue Recruiter" icon="chrome" horizontal href="https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/enfue-recruiter/bcekhmicopoanpjmnjhljkfchoklepch">
  The official extension on the Chrome Web Store.
</Card>

<Warning>
  Only install the extension from this link. It's the official **Enfue Recruiter** extension, published by Enfue. Other extensions with similar names aren't made by Enfue.
</Warning>

## What you can do with it

| You want to | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Add one candidate with their full details, including email and phone | [Add a candidate from their profile](#add-a-candidate-from-their-profile) |
| Add many people quickly while scrolling LinkedIn | [Add people from a list](#add-people-from-a-list) |
| Add someone when you only have their LinkedIn link | [Add by LinkedIn URL](#add-by-linkedin-url) |

## Install and open the extension

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add it to Chrome">
    Open [Enfue Recruiter on the Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/enfue-recruiter/bcekhmicopoanpjmnjhljkfchoklepch) and click **Add to Chrome**, then confirm. You can also get there from Enfue: click **Add applicants**, open the **enfue Chrome** tab, and click **Open Google Chrome Extension**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pin it">
    Click the **Extensions** (puzzle piece) icon in Chrome's toolbar and pin **Enfue Recruiter**, so it's always one click away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Make sure you're signed in to Enfue in the same Chrome browser. The extension adds candidates to the workspace you're signed in to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the side panel">
    Go to LinkedIn and click the Enfue Recruiter icon. The **Enfue Recruiter** panel opens on the right of the page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### The side panel

- **The header** shows your Enfue workspace. Click **View All Applicants** to open your [Applicants](/hiring/applicants/overview) list in Enfue.
- **Profile** tab: a form for adding one person with their details.
- **Import from LinkedIn** tab: a list for adding several people at once.

Once the extension is installed, LinkedIn also shows an **\+ Add to Enfue** button on profiles and in lists of people, such as your **connections**.

## Add a candidate from their profile

Use this when you want a complete record, with email and phone number.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the person's LinkedIn profile">
    Open their profile on LinkedIn, then click **\+ Add to Enfue** next to their name, or open the extension's **Profile** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open their contact info">
    On LinkedIn, click **Contact info** under the person's name. This lets the extension read the email and phone number they share with you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pull their details">
    In the **Profile** tab, click **Pull from LinkedIn**. Enfue fills in their photo, **Name**, **Title** (their LinkedIn headline), **Email**, **Phone number**, and **Social Link**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check and complete the form">
    Correct anything that's wrong, and fill in what's missing. **Name** is the only required field. To start over, click **Clear form**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add them">
    Click **Add to enfue**. The candidate appears in your [Applicants](/hiring/applicants/overview) list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Email and phone number only come through if the person shares them in their LinkedIn **Contact info**, which is usually only visible to their 1st-degree connections. If the fields stay empty, add the details yourself later from their [applicant profile](/hiring/applicants/applicant-profile).
</Tip>

## Add people from a list

Use this to source quickly, for example from your **My Network → Connections** page or from LinkedIn search results.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Import from LinkedIn tab">
    In the extension panel, click **Import from LinkedIn**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where they should go">
    At the top of the tab, pick the **job** they're for and the **talent pool** to add them to. New people you queue up use these choices.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click + Add to Enfue next to each person">
    On the LinkedIn page, click **\+ Add to Enfue** next to each person you want. The button changes to **Added**, and the person appears as a card in the extension panel with their photo, name, and headline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust each person if needed">
    Each card has its own job and talent pool, so you can change them person by person. Click **×** on a card to remove someone, or **Clear all** to empty the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add them to Enfue">
    Click **Add** on each card to add that person to Enfue.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  People added from a list come in with what's shown on LinkedIn: their name, photo, headline, and profile link. To pull in their email and phone number too, use [Add a candidate from their profile](#add-a-candidate-from-their-profile).
</Note>

## Add by LinkedIn URL

In the **Import from LinkedIn** tab, paste the person's profile link, for example `https://www.linkedin.com/in/username`, into **LinkedIn URL**. Pick the job and talent pool, then click **Add**.

You can do the same from inside Enfue, without the extension. See [Add from LinkedIn](/hiring/applicants/add-applicants#add-from-linkedin).

## After you add someone

- The candidate appears in [Applicants](/hiring/applicants/overview), and in the job and [talent pool](/hiring/talent-pools) you chose.
- Like every applicant in a job, they get an [AI match score](/hiring/applicants/ai-review) for the job you picked.
- If the same person ends up in Enfue twice, you can clean it up in [Duplicates](/hiring/jobs/duplicates).

<Tip>
  Contacting people you sourced? Treat their details with care, and only reach out about real opportunities. A short, personal message works far better than a template.
</Tip>
