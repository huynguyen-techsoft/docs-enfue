**Enfue Recruiter** is an extension for Google Chrome. It lets you add candidates you find on the web straight into Enfue, without copying their details by hand.

## Install the extension

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Chrome Web Store">
    Open [Enfue Recruiter on the Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/enfue-recruiter/bcekhmicopoanpjmnjhljkfchoklepch). You can also get there from Enfue: click **Add applicants**, open the **enfue Chrome** tab, and click **Open Google Chrome Extension**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it to Chrome">
    Click **Add to Chrome**, then confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For the other ways to add candidates, see [Adding applicants](/hiring/applicants/add-applicants).
