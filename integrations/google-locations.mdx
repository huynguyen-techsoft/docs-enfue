When you type an address or city in Enfue, Google Locations suggests matching places, so you pick an exact, correctly spelled location instead of typing it out in full. There's nothing to set up.

## Where you'll see it

- **Job locations**, when you [create or edit a job](/hiring/jobs/create-a-job).
- **Your company's addresses**, in [Contact](/settings/general/contact#locations).
- **Interview venues**, when you [schedule an interview](/hiring/interviews#schedule-an-interview). Enfue also suggests your own offices first.

Accurate locations also make maps and directions work properly. See [Google Maps](/integrations/google-maps).
