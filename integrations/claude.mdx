Enfue uses **Claude**, made by Anthropic, alongside [OpenAI](/integrations/openai) to power its AI features. There's nothing to set up: the AI features are built into Enfue.

These include AI Review and match scores, **Create JD for me**, Fil your AI assistant, and AI interviews. See [OpenAI](/integrations/openai#ai-features-in-enfue) for the full list.

<Note>
  AI suggestions help you decide faster, but your team makes the hiring decisions. Always review AI scores and summaries alongside the CV and your interviewers' [evaluations](/hiring/evaluations).
</Note>

## Ask Claude about these docs

At the top of any page in this Help Center, open the page menu and choose **Open in Claude** to ask questions about that page. You can also connect these docs to Claude, Cursor, or VS Code through the **MCP** option in the same menu.
