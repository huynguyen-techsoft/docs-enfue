The **Calendar** shows all your interviews in one place, for your whole team. Use it to see what's coming up, find a free slot before you book an interview, and avoid double-booking your interviewers.

Open **Calendar** from the main menu.

## Choose a view

Click **Month**, **Week**, or **Day** at the top right:

| View | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| **Month** | Seeing the whole month at once, for example how busy the coming weeks are. Each day lists its appointments, and **+4 more** means there are more than fit. Switch to **Week** or **Day** view to see them all. |
| **Week** | Planning interviews. You see each day of the week side by side, hour by hour, so free slots stand out. |
| **Day** | A detailed look at a single day, with every appointment laid out by the hour. |

## Move around the calendar

- Use the back and forward arrows to move to the previous or next month, week, or day, and **Today** to jump back to the current date. The period you're looking at, for example **October 2026**, **October 05 – 11**, or **Monday Oct 05**, appears next to the arrows.
- Today's date is highlighted so you can find it at a glance.

## Read an appointment

Each appointment shows its title, for example the applicant's name for an interview, and the avatars of the people involved. Its position and height show when it starts and how long it lasts. Click an appointment to open its details.

### Show or hide team members' appointments

The avatars at the top right are the team members who organize interviews. Click an avatar to show or hide that person's appointments, for example to see only your own interviews, or to check one interviewer's week before booking them.

## Book an appointment

Click **Create new appointment** at the top right. Pick the applicant, date and time, interviewers, and whether it's in person or a video call, then save. See [Schedule an interview](/hiring/interviews#schedule-an-interview) for each field.

<Tip>
  Check the calendar in **Week** view before you book. You'll see straight away when your interviewers are already busy.
</Tip>

## Sync your calendar

If you've connected your **Google Calendar** or **Outlook** calendar to Enfue, your meetings from it appear here alongside your interviews, such as team stand-ups, sprint reviews, and personal appointments. Click **Sync** to pull in the latest changes.

Because everything is in one place, the free slots you see are really free, and you won't book an interview on top of another meeting.
