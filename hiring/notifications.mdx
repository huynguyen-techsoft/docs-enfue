**Notifications** tell you when something happens that you may need to act on, so you don't have to keep checking every page. The number next to the title, for example **Notifications (3628)**, shows how many notifications you have.

Open **Notifications** from the main menu. The newest notifications are at the top, each with how long ago it happened, for example **2h ago**.

## What you're notified about

### Applicants

| Notification | When you get it | Where to follow up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **New applicant** | Someone applies to one of your jobs, for example **Minh Tran just applied for Senior Backend Developer position**. | [Tasks](/hiring/tasks) or [Applicants](/hiring/applicants/overview). |
| **New open applicant** | Someone applies without choosing a specific job. | [Applicants](/hiring/applicants/overview). |
| **Applicants reached** | One of your jobs reaches a certain number of applicants. | The job's [Applicants](/hiring/jobs/job-applicants) tab. |
| **Proposed applicant** | One of Enfue's talent partners proposes an applicant for one of your jobs. | The job's [TalentSpike](/hiring/jobs/talentspike) tab. |
| **Import completed** | An applicant import you started has finished. | The job's [Applicants](/hiring/jobs/job-applicants) tab. See [Data migration](/settings/general/data-migration). |

### Interviews and evaluations

| Notification | When you get it | Where to follow up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **New evaluation** | An interviewer submits an evaluation, for example **Ben Tran has completed the evaluation for your application**. | The applicant's profile or [Evaluations](/hiring/evaluations). |
| **Interview RSVP updated** | Someone accepts or declines an interview invitation. | [Calendar](/hiring/calendar) or [Interviews](/hiring/interviews). |
| **AI call scheduled** | An [AI interview](/hiring/applicants/applicant-profile#ai-interview) call is scheduled. | The applicant's profile. |

### Emails

| Notification | When you get it | Where to follow up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **New email** | An applicant emails your team, for example **You have received a new email with subject Re: Interview invitation for Senior Backend Developer**. | [Inbox](/hiring/inbox). |

### Jobs and your career page

| Notification | When you get it | Where to follow up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Job post created** | A job post is created. | [Jobs](/hiring/jobs/overview). |
| **Likes and comments** | Someone likes or comments on one of your posts. | The **Posts** tab of your [career page](/workplace/career-page#posts). |
| **Replies** | Someone replies to one of your comments. | The **Posts** tab of your [career page](/workplace/career-page#posts). |

### Billing

| Notification | When you get it | Where to follow up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Subscription** | There's an update about your subscription. | [Subscription](/payments/subscription). |
| **Payment due** | A payment is coming due. | [Invoices](/payments/invoices). |

Click a notification to go straight to it.

## Mark notifications as read

Notifications you haven't read yet are highlighted. Click the **double tick** icon next to the title to mark them all as read at once.

<Tip>
  Notifications build up quickly when you're hiring for several jobs. Mark them all as read at the end of each day, so tomorrow you only see what's new.
</Tip>
