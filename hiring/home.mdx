**Home** is the first screen you see when you sign in to Enfue. It greets you by name and gives you a quick picture of your day, so you know where to start.

Each section has an **open** icon at its top right. Click it to go to the related page for the full details.

## Today at a glance

Three cards across the top show what's happened **Today**:

| Card | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **New Applicants** | How many applicants arrived today. |
| **Interviews** | How many interviews are scheduled for today. See [Interviews](/hiring/interviews). |
| **Evaluations** | How many evaluations were submitted today. See [Evaluations](/hiring/evaluations). |

## Interviews

Shows today's interviews. If there aren't any, click **Schedule Interview** to book one. See [Schedule an interview](/hiring/interviews#schedule-an-interview).

## Recent Activities

A live feed of what's happening in your workspace, grouped by date with the newest first. For example:

- **Anna Le performed AddApplicant on Linh Nguyen**: a team member added an applicant.
- **Minh Tran has applied for Senior Backend Developer**: a candidate applied for a job.

Click a name or a job in the feed to open it.

## Today's Queue

The applicants waiting for action today, with their **Name**, **Job**, **Stage**, **Updated Date**, and **Assignee**. Enfue builds the queue for you automatically each day. Click a column heading to sort by it. Work through the queue to keep applicants moving through your [pipeline](/hiring/pipeline). For your full list with one-click decisions, open [Tasks](/hiring/tasks).

## Recent Applicants

The latest applicants to arrive, with their **Name**, the **Job** they applied for, and when they were added (**Created Date**). Applicants added without a job show **-** in the **Job** column.

<Tip>
  Hiring goes faster as a team. Click **Invite your team member** in the bar at the bottom of the screen to bring colleagues into Enfue, so they can evaluate applicants, schedule interviews, and share the work.
</Tip>
