The **Jobs** page is where you create roles, see how each one is performing, and work through its applicants. Open it from **Hiring → Jobs** in the main menu.

The page has two parts:

- **The job list** on the left, with every job in your workspace.
- **The job workspace** on the right, showing the job you selected in the list.

<Tip>
Use the arrow button between the two panels to collapse the job list and give the job workspace the full width of the screen.
</Tip>

## The job list

At the top of the list you'll find:

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Jobs / Internship** tabs | Switch between regular jobs and internship positions. Internships work exactly like jobs, with all the same features; keeping them in a separate tab just makes them easier to find. |
| **Create job** | Start a new job. See [Creating and editing a job](/hiring/jobs/create-a-job). |
| **Search jobs** | Find a job by its title. |
| **Active / Archived** | Show jobs that are open, or jobs you have archived. Active is the default. |
| **Location** | Show only the jobs in a given location. |
| **Status** | Filter the list by job status. |

### Reading a job card

Each job in the list appears as a card showing:

- **Job title**
- **Employment type**, such as FullTime or PartTime
- **Location**, such as Da Nang
- **When the job was created**, for example "4 days ago"
- **Visibility badge**:
  - **Public** (green): the job is published on your career page and candidates can apply.
  - **Internal** (orange): the job is for internal use only and does not appear on your career page.
- **Three counters** in the bottom-right corner:
  - **People icon**: the number of applicants.
  - **Eye icon**: the number of times the job has been viewed.
  - **Person icon**: the number of people hired.

### Job card menu

Click the **⋮** menu on a card for these actions:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Copy link** | Copy the job's public link, ready to share. |
| **Kanban view** | See the job's applicants as cards in stage columns, like the [Pipeline](/hiring/pipeline). |
| **View as candidate** | See the job details exactly as candidates see them. |
| **Refresh** | Bump the job so it shows as newly updated. |
| **Archive** | Close the job. It stops accepting applications and moves to the **Archived** list. |

## Inside a job

Click a job card to open it. The job title appears at the top, with these tabs underneath:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Applicants" icon="users" href="/hiring/jobs/job-applicants">
    Everyone who applied, with their stage, evaluations, and AI match score.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Duplicates" icon="clone" href="/hiring/jobs/duplicates">
    Applicants who appear more than once, so you can clean them up.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Report" icon="chart-line" href="/hiring/jobs/job-report">
    Applicant numbers, stage conversion, sources, and disqualification reasons.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Integrations" icon="share-nodes" href="/hiring/jobs/job-integrations">
    Share the job and collect applicants from LinkedIn, job boards, email, and your website.
  </Card>
  <Card title="TalentSpike" icon="bolt" href="/hiring/jobs/talentspike">
    Qualified candidates from Enfue's talent partners that you can add to your job.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Edit" icon="pen" href="/hiring/jobs/create-a-job">
    Change the job details, description, skills, and visibility.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Data Migration" icon="file-import" href="/hiring/jobs/data-migration">
    Bulk import resumes or a candidate spreadsheet into this job.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Talent Discovery" icon="wand-magic-sparkles" href="/hiring/jobs/talent-discovery">
    Let Fil, your AI assistant, find matching candidates in your talent pools.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Two buttons sit in the top-right corner of every job:

- **View Applicant Form** opens the application form candidates fill in for this job, so you can see it the way they do.
- The **copy** icon next to it copies the job's link.
