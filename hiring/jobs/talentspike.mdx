**TalentSpike** brings you qualified candidates you haven't met yet. Enfue's talent partners provide a shortlist of candidates who fit your job. You can review them, and when you find someone you like, **acquire** them to add them to your own applicants.

To unlock support from talent partners, top up your company's [Wallet](/payments/wallet).

Use TalentSpike when your own channels aren't bringing in enough strong applicants, or when you need to fill a role quickly.

## Review TalentSpike candidates

1. Open the job from **Hiring → Jobs**.
2. Click the **TalentSpike** tab.
3. Review the candidates Enfue's talent partners have provided for this job.

## Acquire a candidate

When you acquire a candidate, they become one of your applicants for this job. They then appear in the job's [Applicants](/hiring/jobs/job-applicants) tab and on your [Pipeline](/hiring/pipeline), where you can work with them like any other applicant.

<Tip>
TalentSpike candidates come from Enfue's talent partners. Candidates you already have are found with [Talent Discovery](/hiring/jobs/talent-discovery), which searches your own talent pools.
</Tip>
