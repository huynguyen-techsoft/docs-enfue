title: "Import applicants into a job"
sidebarTitle: "Data Migration"
description: "Bulk import applicants straight into one job from resume PDFs, a candidate spreadsheet, or a ZIP of CVs and evaluations."
icon: "file-import"
---

Every job has a **Data Migration** tab for adding many applicants to that job at once, for example a batch of CVs from a recruitment agency.

Open a job from **Hiring → Jobs** and click the **Data Migration** tab. It offers the same three import types as **Settings → Data Migration**: **Resume PDFs**, **Candidate Spreadsheet**, and **CVs & Evaluations**. The only difference is that the job is already chosen for you. Imported applicants appear in the job's [Applicants](/hiring/jobs/job-applicants) tab.

For step-by-step instructions on each import type, including the spreadsheet template and the folder layout for CVs & Evaluations, see [Data migration](/settings/general/data-migration).

<Tip>
After a large import, check the job's [Duplicates](/hiring/jobs/duplicates) tab for candidates who were already in Enfue.
</Tip>
