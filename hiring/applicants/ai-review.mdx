Every applicant gets an **AI Review**: a match score out of 100 that shows how well they fit the job, a breakdown of where the score comes from, and a short summary written by AI. It helps you decide who to look at first, especially when a job attracts hundreds of applicants.

## Where you'll see the match score

- As a colored bar in the **Match Score** column of [Applicants](/hiring/applicants/overview) and [Tasks](/hiring/tasks).
- In a circle on each applicant card on [mobile](/mobile/applicants).
- In the **Evaluations** section of the [applicant profile](/hiring/applicants/applicant-profile#ai-review), and as a label next to the applicant's name, for example **45% Low Match**.
- On the evaluation page, so interviewers see it too. See [Evaluations](/hiring/evaluations#completing-an-evaluation-for-interviewers).

A dash instead of a score means the applicant hasn't been scored yet.

## What the score is made of

The match score adds up five factors. Expand the **AI Review** to see them on a chart and as a **Factor breakdown**:

| Factor | Points | What it looks at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Skills Match** | up to 30 | How well the applicant's skills match the skills the job needs. |
| **Relevant Experience** | up to 30 | How relevant their work experience is to the role. |
| **Role Fit** | up to 20 | How well their background fits the role overall. |
| **Qualifications** | up to 10 | Their education and qualifications. |
| **Practical Fit** | up to 10 | Practical points, such as location and availability. |

Below the breakdown, the **AI summary** explains the applicant's strengths and any gaps in a few sentences.

<Tip>
  The score is only as good as the job description. Add the required skills and a clear description when you [create the job](/hiring/jobs/create-a-job), so the AI knows what to look for.
</Tip>

## Use it well

- **Sort by match score** to review the strongest applicants first.
- **Read the breakdown, not just the number.** An applicant with a low **Skills Match** but full marks for **Relevant Experience** may still be worth a call.
- **Combine it with your team's judgment.** The score helps you prioritize; your interviewers' [evaluations](/hiring/evaluations) decide.

<Note>
  AI scores can miss context a person would notice, such as a career change or transferable skills. Always review the CV before disqualifying someone.
</Note>

The AI features in Enfue are powered by [OpenAI](/integrations/openai) and [Claude](/integrations/claude).
