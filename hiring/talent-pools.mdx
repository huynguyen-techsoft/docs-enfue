A talent pool is a named group of applicants you want to keep track of, such as strong runners-up for a role, graduates from a campus event, or everyone with a particular skill. A pool isn't tied to one job: it can hold applicants from any of your jobs, and applicants who never applied for a job at all.

Open **Talent Pools** from the main menu. Your pools are listed on the left, each with its number of applicants and when it was last updated. Use **Search** at the top of the list to find a pool by name, and click the arrow on the edge of the list to collapse or expand it.

<Tip>
  Talent pools are also where [Talent Discovery](/hiring/jobs/talent-discovery) looks when Fil, your AI assistant, searches for candidates who fit a new job. The more you keep your pools up to date, the more Fil can find for you.
</Tip>

## Create a talent pool

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new pool">
    Click **Create** at the top of the **Talent Pools** list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Enter a **Title**. This is required. Pick a name that says who belongs in the pool, for example **Top Business Analysts** or **Data Engineer Interns 2026**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe it (optional)">
    Add a **Description** of up to 1,000 characters, for example who should be added and why.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The new pool appears in the list, ready for applicants.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add applicants to a pool

There are four ways to put applicants in a pool:

| Where | How |
| --- | --- |
| **In the pool** | Open the pool and click **Add applicants**. |
| **On the applicant's profile** | On the **Contact** tab of the [full profile](/hiring/applicants/applicant-profile#profile-details), click **\+** next to **Talent Pools**. |
| **For many applicants at once** | Select applicants on the **Applicants** page and choose **Add to Talent Pools**. See [Bulk actions](/hiring/applicants/bulk-actions). |
| **When you add a new applicant** | Pick one or more pools in the **Talent Pools** field. See [Adding applicants](/hiring/applicants/add-applicants). |

An applicant can be in as many pools as you like.

## Work with a pool

Click a pool in the list to open it. Each pool has three tabs: **Applicants**, **Report**, and **Edit**.

### Applicants

The **Applicants** tab lists everyone in the pool.

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | The applicant's name. |
| **Job** | The job they applied for, if any. |
| **Stage** | Their current hiring stage, for example **Applied**. |
| **Status** | **Active** or **Inactive**. |
| **Source** | Where the applicant came from. |
| **Assignee** | The team member responsible for them. |

Above the list, a row of boxes shows how many applicants in the pool are at each of your hiring stages, for example **Applied**, **Matching Interview**, **Ability Interview**, **Offering**, and **Hired**. Click a box to show only the applicants at that stage.

#### Filter the pool

Narrow the list with **Search**, **Jobs**, **Stages**, and **Assignees**. Click **Filters** for more options:

- **Active applicants**, **Inactive applicants**, **Flagged applicants**, and **Applicants with evaluations**
- **Tags**: applicants with a particular [tag](/hiring/applicants/tags)
- **Source**: where applicants came from
- **Applied Duration** and **Updated Duration**: applicants who applied, or were last updated, between two dates

The number next to **Stages** or **Filters** shows how many filters are in use, and the count on the right, for example **6/6 applicants**, shows how many applicants match out of everyone in the pool. Click **Clear all** to remove every filter. If nobody matches, the list shows **No data**.

<Tip>
  Combine a pool with tags for fast shortlists. For example, open **Top Business Analysts** and filter by the tag `available-now` to see who you could approach for an urgent role.
</Tip>

### Report

The **Report** tab shows how the pool is growing and where its applicants are:

| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Total Applicants** | Everyone in the pool. |
| **New This Month** | Applicants added this month. |
| **Average Match Score** | The average AI match score of the applicants in the pool. See [Understand the AI match score](/hiring/applicants/ai-review). |
| **Talent Pool Growth** | How many applicants joined the pool each month. |
| **Source by Channel** | Where the pool's applicants came from. |
| **Source by Assignee** | How the pool's applicants are split between assignees. |
| **Hiring Pipeline Funnel** | How many of the pool's applicants are at each hiring stage. |

### Edit

On the **Edit** tab, change the pool's **Title** or **Description** and click **Save**.

## Tips for useful pools

- **Make pools for people, not jobs.** Jobs open and close, but a pool like **Strong .NET developers** stays useful for every future .NET role.
- **Add people when you meet them.** When a good applicant isn't right for this job, add them to a pool before you [disqualify](/hiring/disqualify) them, so they're easy to find later.
- **Use the description.** Write down who belongs in the pool so your whole team adds people the same way.
- **Use tags for details, pools for groups.** A pool is a list you come back to. [Tags](/hiring/applicants/tags) describe things about individual applicants, such as availability, that you filter by within a pool.
