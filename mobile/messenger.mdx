---

Tap **Messenger** in the menu at the bottom of the screen, or the **chat** icon at the top.

You'll see your chats with candidates, each with the latest message and when it was sent.

- Tap a chat to open it and reply.
- Tap the **compose** icon to start a new chat.

You can only chat with candidates who have an Enfue account. For everyone else, use the [Inbox](/mobile/inbox). See [Messenger](/hiring/messenger).

<Tip>
  A quick message is a good nudge after an important email, for example to let a candidate know their offer is waiting.
</Tip>
