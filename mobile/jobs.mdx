Tap **Jobs** in the menu at the bottom of the screen.

## Find a job

- Switch between the **Jobs** and **Internship** tabs.
- Tap **All**, **Public**, or **Internal** to filter them.
- Use **Search jobs** and the filter buttons to narrow the list.

## What each job shows

Each job shows its title, employment type, location, and when it was posted, whether it's **Public** or **Internal**, and its activity counts, such as how many applicants it has.

Tap **⋮** on a job for more options.

## Create a job

Tap **Create job**. See [Create and edit a job](/hiring/jobs/create-a-job).
