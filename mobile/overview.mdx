Enfue doesn't have a mobile app yet, but you don't need one. Enfue works well in the browser on your phone or tablet, with a layout made for small screens, so you can review applicants, reply to emails, chat with candidates, and keep an eye on your jobs wherever you are.

## Open Enfue on your phone

1. Open your phone's browser, such as Safari or Chrome.
2. Go to [app.enfue.com](https://app.enfue.com).
3. Sign in with your usual Enfue account.

<Tip>
  To open Enfue with one tap, add it to your home screen. In Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. In Chrome on Android, tap the **⋮** menu, then **Add to Home screen**.
</Tip>

## Find your way around

On mobile, Enfue has a bar at the top and a menu at the bottom of the screen.

**At the top:**

- **Search**, to find applicants and more.
- The **chat** icon, which opens [Messenger](/hiring/messenger).
- The **bell**, which opens your [Notifications](/hiring/notifications). The red badge shows how many are unread, for example **99+**.

**At the bottom**, tap to switch between the five screens you use most:

| Tab | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Today's numbers and interviews. |
| **Inbox** | Read and send applicant emails. |
| **Messenger** | Chat with candidates. |
| **Applicants** | Browse and review applicants. |
| **Jobs** | Check and create jobs. |

## Home

The same [Home](/hiring/home) screen as on a computer, arranged in one column: **New Applicants**, **Interviews**, and **Evaluations** for today at the top, then today's interviews with **Schedule Interview** below.

## Inbox

Tap **Inbox** to see your team's email conversations, newest first. Tap a conversation to open the applicant:

- At the top: their name, application date, evaluation counts, and email address. Tap **Back** to return to the list.
- **Applied job**, **Current stage**, and **Assignee**. Tap any of them to change it.
- Their **Phone number**.
- **View applicant detail**, **Disqualify**, and **Proceed further**.
- **Email history**: every email in the conversation. Tap one to read it.

To reply, tap **New Email**. The email form opens full screen, with the same options as on a computer: **Use email template**, **CC**, **Subject**, **Append previous email**, **Attach file**, and **Send**. See [Inbox](/hiring/inbox#reply-to-an-applicant).

## Messenger

Tap **Messenger** to see your chats with candidates, with the latest message and when it was sent. Tap a chat to open it, or tap the **compose** icon to start a new one. See [Messenger](/hiring/messenger).

## Applicants

Tap **Applicants** to browse applicants as cards. Each card shows:

- The applicant's name and current stage.
- **Applied for**: the job they applied for.
- Their **match score** in a circle, out of 100.
- How long ago they applied, and their source, such as **LinkedIn** or **Facebook**.

Swipe the row of stages at the top, such as **Applied** and **Matching Interview**, and tap one to see only the applicants in it. Use **Search** and the **filter** button to narrow the list; the number on the filter button shows how many filters are on. Tap the **\+** button at the bottom right to add an applicant. See [Applicants](/hiring/applicants/overview).

## Jobs

Tap **Jobs** to see your jobs. Switch between the **Jobs** and **Internship** tabs, and tap **All**, **Public**, or **Internal** to filter them.

Each job shows its title, employment type, location, when it was posted, whether it's **Public** or **Internal**, and its activity counts, such as how many applicants it has. Tap **⋮** on a job for more options, or **Create job** to add a new one. See [Jobs](/hiring/jobs/overview).

## Evaluation links work on mobile too

When someone sends you an [evaluation link](/hiring/evaluations#request-an-evaluation) in Zalo, Slack, or Teams, tap it to open the evaluation in your phone's browser and submit it there.
