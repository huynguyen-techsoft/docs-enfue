Enfue doesn't have a mobile app yet, but you don't need one. Enfue works well in the browser on your phone or tablet, with a layout made for small screens, so you can review applicants, reply to emails, chat with candidates, and keep an eye on your jobs wherever you are.

## What do you want to do on your phone?

| I want to… | Go to |
| --- | --- |
| See today's new applicants, interviews, and evaluations | [Check your day](/mobile/home) |
| Book an interview while I'm away from my desk | [Check your day](/mobile/home) |
| Reply to an applicant's email | [Reply to applicant emails](/mobile/inbox#reply) |
| Move an applicant forward, or disqualify them, after they reply | [Reply to applicant emails](/mobile/inbox#open-a-conversation) |
| Send a candidate a quick message | [Chat with candidates](/mobile/messenger) |
| Look up an applicant and check their match score | [Find and add applicants](/mobile/applicants#find-applicants) |
| Add a candidate I just met at an event | [Find and add applicants](/mobile/applicants#add-an-applicant) |
| Check how my jobs are doing | [Check and create jobs](/mobile/jobs#find-a-job) |
| Post a new job | [Check and create jobs](/mobile/jobs#create-a-job) |
| Evaluate a candidate from a link someone sent me | [Evaluate from a link](#evaluate-from-a-link) |
| See my notifications | Tap the **bell** at the top. See [Notifications](/hiring/notifications). |

## Get set up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Enfue in your browser">
    Open your phone's browser, such as Safari or Chrome, and go to [app.enfue.com](https://app.enfue.com).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Sign in with your usual Enfue account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Enfue to your home screen">
    So you can open Enfue with one tap. In Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. In Chrome on Android, tap the **⋮** menu, then **Add to Home screen**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Find your way around

**At the top of the screen:**

- **Search**, to find applicants and more.
- The **chat** icon, which opens [Messenger](/mobile/messenger).
- The **bell**, which opens your [Notifications](/hiring/notifications). The red badge shows how many are unread.

**At the bottom of the screen**, tap to switch between the five screens you use most:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Check your day" icon="house" href="/mobile/home">
    **Home**: today's numbers and interviews.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Reply to applicant emails" icon="inbox" href="/mobile/inbox">
    **Inbox**: read and reply to emails, and move applicants forward.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Chat with candidates" icon="messages" href="/mobile/messenger">
    **Messenger**: quick messages to candidates with an Enfue account.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Find and add applicants" icon="users" href="/mobile/applicants">
    **Applicants**: browse, filter, and add applicants.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Check and create jobs" icon="briefcase" href="/mobile/jobs">
    **Jobs**: see how your jobs are doing, and post new ones.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Evaluate from a link

When someone sends you an [evaluation link](/hiring/evaluations#request-an-evaluation) in Zalo, Slack, or Teams, tap it to open the evaluation in your phone's browser. Rate the applicant, choose a recommendation, write your note, and submit it there. See [Completing an evaluation](/hiring/evaluations#completing-an-evaluation-for-interviewers).
