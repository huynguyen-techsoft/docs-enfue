Tap **Inbox** in the menu at the bottom of the screen to see your team's email conversations, newest first.

## Open a conversation

Tap a conversation to open the applicant:

- At the top: their name, application date, evaluation counts, and email address. Tap **Back** to return to the list.
- **Applied job**, **Current stage**, and **Assignee**. Tap any of them to change it.
- Their **Phone number**.
- **View applicant detail**, **Disqualify**, and **Proceed further**.
- **Email history**: every email in the conversation. Tap one to read it.

## Reply

Tap **New Email**. The email form opens full screen, with the same options as on a computer: **Use email template**, **CC**, **Subject**, **Append previous email**, **Attach file**, and **Send**. See [Reply to an applicant](/hiring/inbox#reply-to-an-applicant).

<Tip>
  Use an [email template](/settings/applications/email-templates) to reply quickly on your phone, without typing long messages.
</Tip>
