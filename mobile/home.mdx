Tap **Home** in the menu at the bottom of the screen.

You'll see the same [Home](/hiring/home) screen as on a computer, arranged in one column:

- **New Applicants**, **Interviews**, and **Evaluations** for today, at the top.
- **Today's interviews** below. If there aren't any, tap **Schedule Interview** to book one.

<Tip>
  Open Home first thing in the morning to see what's waiting for you today.
</Tip>
