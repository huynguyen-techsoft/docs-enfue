Whenever you upgrade your plan, top up your wallet, or pay an invoice, you'll go through Enfue's **Secure Checkout**.

## Payment methods

| Method | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **VietQR** | Scan a QR code with your Vietnamese banking app. |
| **PayPal** | Pay with your PayPal account. |
| **Visa** or **Mastercard** | Pay by credit or debit card. |

## Pay

1. Pick your payment method under **Secure Checkout**.
2. Click **Continue to payment**.
3. Complete the payment with your bank, PayPal, or card.

Once your payment goes through, its invoice is marked **Paid** under [Invoices](/payments/invoices).

<Tip>
  If a payment doesn't go through, the invoice stays **Unpaid**. Click **Pay** next to it under [Invoices](/payments/invoices) to try again.
</Tip>
