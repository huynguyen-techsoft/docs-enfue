Your **Wallet** holds prepaid credit for your company. Topping it up unlocks support from Enfue's **talent partners**, who provide qualified candidates for your jobs. You receive those candidates in each job's [TalentSpike](/hiring/jobs/talentspike) tab.

The **Wallet balance** at the top shows how much credit you have.

## Top up your wallet

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start">
    Click **Top up**. The **Top-up wallet** window shows your **Current balance**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter an amount">
    Under **Amount to top up**, enter how much to add. The window shows the minimum amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Accept**, then complete the [payment](/payments/payment).
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each top-up creates an invoice, listed as **TalentFlow - TopUp** under [Invoices](/payments/invoices). If you start a top-up but don't finish paying, its invoice stays **Unpaid**, and you can pay it from there later.
