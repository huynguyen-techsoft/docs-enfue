**Subscription** shows which Enfue plan your company is on, how much of it you're using, and lets you change plans.

## Subscription details

At the top, you'll see:

- **Your current subscription**, for example **Professional**.
- **Subscribed on**: when your plan started.
- **Cycle**: whether you pay **Monthly** or **Annually**.
- **Next Payment due date**: when your next payment is due.

### Available resources

Bars show how much of your plan's allowance you've used:

| Resource | Example | What it counts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Total Applicants Storage** | **2,099/10,000** | Applicants stored in your workspace. |
| **Total Admin Accounts** | **4/5** | [Admin](/settings/users/admins) seats in use. |

<Tip>
  Check these bars before a big hiring campaign or a [data migration](/settings/general/data-migration). If you're close to a limit, upgrade first so nothing gets held up.
</Tip>

## Compare plans

Under **Select a plan**, compare the three plans side by side. Switch between **Annually** and **Monthly** to see both prices. Paying annually saves 17%.

| | Flow Lite | Flow Professional | Flow Enterprise |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Company recruiters** (admin seats) | 2 | 5 | Unlimited |
| **Interviewers** | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| **Applicants storage** | 1,000 | 10,000 | Unlimited |
| **Public, internship, and internal jobs** | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| **Talent Pools** | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |

Every plan includes the Applicant Tracking System and: an Employer Branding page, applicant management, event management, Office and Google calendar connections, who viewed your jobs, messaging, the AI chat bot, and video CVs.

Your current plan is marked **Current Plan**. Prices are shown in Enfue, in US dollars (USD). For **Flow Enterprise**, click **Contact us** to talk to our team.

## Change your plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Under **Select a plan**, click the button on the plan you want, for example **Upgrade** or **Downgrade**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how often to pay">
    Pick **Annually** or **Monthly**. The annual price is shown both per month and per year.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to pay">
    Under **Secure Checkout**, pick **VietQR**, **PayPal**, or a **Visa** or **Mastercard** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    Click **Continue to payment** and complete the payment. Your invoice appears under [Invoices](/payments/invoices).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  A downgrade takes effect at the end of your current billing cycle, so you keep your current plan until then. Before you downgrade, check that your admins and stored applicants will fit within the smaller plan's limits.
</Note>
