**Invoices** lists every invoice for your company: plan changes and wallet top-ups.

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Invoice No.** | The invoice number. Quote it if you contact us about a payment. |
| **Description** | What it's for, for example **TalentFlow - PlanChange** for a plan change, or **TalentFlow - TopUp** for a [wallet top-up](/payments/wallet). |
| **Due Date** | When payment is due. |
| **Start date** and **End date** | The period the invoice covers, where relevant. |
| **Paid on** | When it was paid. |
| **Amount** | How much it's for, in US dollars (USD). |
| **Status** | **Paid** or **Unpaid**. |
| **Action** | **Pay**, for invoices that are still unpaid. |

## Pay an invoice

Click **Pay** next to an **Unpaid** invoice, then complete the payment. See [Payment](/payments/payment) for the payment methods.

<Tip>
  An unpaid top-up invoice usually means a top-up was started but not finished. Nothing is added to your wallet until it's paid.
</Tip>

Questions about an invoice? Email [support@enfue.com](mailto:support@enfue.com) with the invoice number.
