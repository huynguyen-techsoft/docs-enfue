<Update label="Jun 29, 2025" description="Kanban board options and in-app notifications">
  - **Kanban board improvements:** view options, more column colors, and assigning all applicants at once. See [Pipeline](/hiring/pipeline).
  - **In-app notifications.** See [Notifications](/hiring/notifications).
  - Internships are shown separately from other jobs.
  - A report of hires per company admin, and a better view of appointments on the dashboard.
  - Add an onboarding date for new hires.
  - Team members can join their company's profile.
  - Post lists work on mobile.
  - Fixed seeing older messages, password reset for social sign-in, and the create job button.
</Update>

<Update label="Jun 22, 2025" description="Applicant filters and calendar organizers">
  - **Filter applicants.**
  - **Filter the calendar by organizer.** See [Calendar](/hiring/calendar).
  - See who viewed or attended.
  - Choose a plan when creating a company, with an improved pricing page.
  - Digital business cards.
  - Richer personal profiles, and easier company switching.
  - Image size options, faster video loading, and many fixes.
</Update>

<Update label="Jun 15, 2025" description="Multiple job locations">
  - A job can have several locations.
  - CSV import has its own page. See [Data migration](/settings/general/data-migration).
  - Related jobs are shown on job pages.
  - A smoother experience for company admins.
  - An improved company details page, and quiz updates.
</Update>

<Update label="Jun 8, 2025" description="Interviewers and large imports">
  - **Import up to 10,000 applicants** at once.
  - **Company interviewers:** add the people who interview for you, including admins. See [Interviewers](/settings/users/interviewers).
  - Videos in posts.
  - A refreshed company profile, business profiles, and meet-up events with member search.
  - Fixed viewing video CVs, archiving jobs, creating jobs, and saving contacts to your phone.
</Update>

<Update label="Jun 1, 2025" description="Online meetings and LinkedIn applicants">
  - **Online meetings** for interviews. See [Interviews](/hiring/interviews).
  - **Add an applicant from their LinkedIn URL.** See [Adding applicants](/hiring/applicants/add-applicants).
  - Jobs show hires and their assignee.
  - Company members show when they last used Enfue.
  - A new flow for inviting applicants to interviews.
  - Quiz notifications, and quizzes work on mobile.
  - You stay signed in longer.
  - Fixed Inbox scrolling, calendar event lists, and last sign-in times.
</Update>
