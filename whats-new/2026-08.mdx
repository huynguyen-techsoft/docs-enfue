<Update label="Aug 30, 2026" description="Bulk CV upload and better evaluation requests">
  - **Upload many CVs at once**, as individual PDF files or a ZIP file. See [Adding applicants](/hiring/applicants/add-applicants).
  - **CC people on evaluation requests**, and evaluation links now open the evaluation directly. See [Request an evaluation](/hiring/evaluations#request-an-evaluation).
  - Interviewers now see your [custom evaluation fields](/settings/applications/custom-fields-for-evaluation) and the applicant's cover letter with its formatting.
  - "Invite interviewers" is now called **Request evaluation**.
  - Edit an interview after you've scheduled it. New interviews last 60 minutes by default.
  - Invite admins who don't have an Enfue account yet. See [Admins](/settings/users/admins#add-an-admin).
  - Resize the columns of the applicant table, which also adapts better to your screen.
  - A new, lighter toolbar for previewing CVs.
  - Shared job links open the public job page.
  - [Notes](/hiring/notes) now use **Discard** and **Save** buttons.
  - Clearer error messages, and faster, more accurate search.
  - Inbox fixes for avatars, job details, and disqualification reasons.
</Update>

<Update label="Aug 23, 2026" description="Hired page, LinkedIn details, and requalify">
  - **New Hired page**, with a report of your hires and each new hire's onboarding state. See [Hired](/hiring/hired).
  - A new **LinkedIn** tab on applicant profiles, where you can add and edit their experience and education.
  - **Requalify** a disqualified applicant, and see an alert on the profiles of disqualified applicants.
  - Applicant details now open in a preview panel, with a polished header, tabs, and preview.
  - [Talent pools](/hiring/talent-pools) get applicant filters and more columns.
  - The screening questions tab shows applicants' answers to your custom fields.
  - Keyword search finds names and words even when typed without Vietnamese accents.
  - The Hired list updates automatically, and the Hired stage has a tooltip.
  - CSV imports clean up email address formatting. Bulk emails skip applicants without an email address.
  - When an account is suspended, adding applicants and search are turned off.
  - Fixed subscription pages when an invoice is missing, and notifications for posts without text.
</Update>

<Update label="Aug 16, 2026" description="A new applicant page">
  - **A new applicant detail page**, with a polished header and sidebar, and **Preview** and **Details** actions in the applicant list. See [The applicant profile](/hiring/applicants/applicant-profile).
  - The stage indicator shows when the applicant reached each stage.
  - The [Pipeline](/hiring/pipeline) hides applicants who haven't been updated for more than 60 days, and each card shows when it was last updated.
  - Bulk emails can use your own [email templates](/settings/applications/email-templates).
  - A polished panel for scheduling interviews, with a new communication option, and refreshed communication panels.
  - **Message us** in the Help menu now opens a chat with our support team. See [Get help](/help/overview).
  - Upload images and media in every editor, with smoother document and editor tools.
  - Your evaluation filter is kept in the page address, so filtered views can be shared.
  - Fixes for talent pool scrolling, column widths, labels, and inline editing.
</Update>

<Update label="Aug 9, 2026" description="Real-time notes, refreshed reports, and simpler filters">
  - **Edit notes together in real time.** Several people can work on the same note at once. See [Notes](/hiring/notes#edit-together-without-losing-anything).
  - **Duplicate and share notes.** See [Notes](/hiring/notes#manage-your-notes).
  - **Refreshed [Reports](/hiring/reports)**, with an improved recruiter performance table and a list of late jobs.
  - **Simpler filters** on the [Pipeline](/hiring/pipeline), [Tasks](/hiring/tasks), and applicant pages, with the same toolbar everywhere and a breakdown by stage.
  - Improved Pipeline cards.
  - Reorder your [custom evaluation fields](/settings/applications/custom-fields-for-evaluation) by dragging them, and a simpler evaluation form.
  - Change an applicant's disqualification reason after disqualifying them.
  - Edit every detail of a scheduled interview, with an easier interview form.
  - An easier form for [adding an applicant manually](/hiring/applicants/add-applicants#add-an-applicant-manually).
  - When the sidebar is collapsed, hover over a section to see its full menu.
  - Fixed email template parameters, source icon uploads, interview time ranges, and flickering filters.
</Update>

<Update label="Aug 2, 2026" description="Disqualification reasons, public notes, and talent pool reports">
  - **Disqualification reasons:** choose why you disqualify an applicant, from a list you manage. See [Disqualified reasons](/settings/applications/disqualified-reasons).
  - **Share notes publicly,** and let readers comment on them. See [Share a note](/hiring/notes#share-a-note).
  - **Talent pool reports and filters:** a new **Report** tab, new filters, and an improved layout. See [Talent Pools](/hiring/talent-pools#report).
  - A new field type for [custom evaluation fields](/settings/applications/custom-fields-for-evaluation), shown in evaluations, with a consistent evaluation form and a clearer evaluation history.
  - More flexible times when scheduling interviews, and a scrollable interview form.
  - Recruiter performance in [Reports](/hiring/reports#team-performance-for-team-leaders).
  - A refreshed Notes sidebar.
  - Job cards and the profile sidebar work better on small screens.
  - Editor, display, and link fixes.
</Update>
