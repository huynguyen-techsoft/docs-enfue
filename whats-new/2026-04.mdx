<Update label="Apr 26, 2026" description="UI updates">
  - Small improvements to the [applicant profile](/hiring/applicants/applicant-profile).
  - Added [custom fields for evaluations](/settings/applications/custom-fields-for-evaluation).
  - The **Stages** filter now lets you pick several stages at once.
</Update>

<Update label="Apr 19, 2026" description="New releases">
  - **Calendar sync** with [Google Calendar](/integrations/google-calendar) and [Microsoft Calendar](/integrations/microsoft-calendar).
  - Sign in with [LinkedIn](/integrations/linkedin).
  - Improved custom fields.
  - Text improvements.
</Update>

<Update label="Apr 12, 2026" description="Changes and fixes">
  - CVs now open in a new tab by default.
  - Improved forms and notifications.
  - Improved custom field integrations.
  - Fixed bugs for Apple users.
</Update>

<Update label="Apr 5, 2026" description="Recruiter updates">
  - Companies can choose whether applicants must sign in to Enfue before applying. See [Enfue Authentication](/integrations/connected-tools#enfue-authentication).
  - Improved direct links.
  - Added a greeting animation.
  - Improved text.
</Update>
