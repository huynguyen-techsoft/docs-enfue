<Update label="May 25, 2025" description="Quizzes and company sign-up">
  - **Quizzes** to engage your community.
  - An updated sign-up for company admins.
  - An improved LinkedIn profile field, and updated Terms and Privacy pages.
  - Fixed the calendar day view showing the wrong day.
</Update>

<Update label="May 18, 2025" description="Calendar and events">
  - **[Calendar](/hiring/calendar):** create, view, edit, and cancel events, and see what's coming up.
  - Emails include the event date and time.
  - Easier company creation, and job activities are sorted by date.
  - Fixed the **Message** and **Follow** buttons on company links.
</Update>

<Update label="May 11, 2025" description="Custom stages and email attachments">
  - **Rename your hiring stages.** See [Applicant stages](/settings/applications/applicant-stages).
  - Attachments in the mailbox.
  - Friendly links for jobs.
  - See when company members last signed in, and add Enfue users by their username.
  - Line breaks in comments, and longer bios on personal profiles.
  - English and Vietnamese in the company admin area and subscription plans.
  - Mobile improvements, and fixed Google sign-in and applying from in-app browsers.
</Update>

<Update label="May 4, 2025" description="Talent pools and the mailbox">
  - **[Talent pools](/hiring/talent-pools):** create, edit, and view pools, and add applicants to them.
  - **The mailbox,** for emailing applicants. See [Inbox](/hiring/inbox).
  - Shorter links for personal and company profiles.
  - Job lists exclude internships, and fixed duplicate jobs.
</Update>
