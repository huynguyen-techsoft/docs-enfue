<Update label="Oct 11, 2026" description="AI interviews, Fil, and applicant improvements">
  - **New: AI interviews.** Prepare your interview questions, let candidates complete the interview with Enfue's AI, and review the results. See [AI interview questions](/settings/applications/ai-interview-questions).
  - **Talent Discovery** now suggests related applicants. See [Talent Discovery](/hiring/jobs/talent-discovery).
  - Hire with **Fil**: a new guided, AI-led flow for finding and hiring candidates, plus a conversational search when you get started.
  - Clearer wording and a smoother experience in Enfue's AI assistants, Fin and Fil.
  - The application form now fully supports your [language labels](/settings/applications/application-form#language-labels), and custom fields appear in the order you created them.
  - The applicant filter now lets you show **active and inactive** applicants together.
  - A clearer toolbar for [bulk actions](/hiring/applicants/bulk-actions).
  - Job details now include a button to open the job's Kanban board, with improved action buttons.
  - Better job search results.
  - Team members who aren't admins or interviewers are now taken straight to the Enfue jobs site, and sign-in carries over your email address.
  - Faster sign-up, with browser autofill on the sign-up form.
  - Fixed evaluation visibility for interviews scheduled from the [Calendar](/hiring/calendar), and added missing labels on job pages.
</Update>

  The Enfue Help Center is here, with step-by-step guides for everything in Enfue: [Get Started](/), [Guides](/hiring/home), [Integrations](/integrations/overview), [Settings](/settings/overview), and [Help](/help/overview), including an [FAQ](/help/faq) and a [glossary](/help/glossary).
</Update>

<Update label="Oct 4, 2026" description="Hiring workspace and a new match score">
  - **New hiring workspace** with an AI talent search flow, and a simpler hiring setup. Hiring now also works for companies outside Enfue.
  - **New match score design**, now also shown in the evaluation form. See [AI Review](/hiring/applicants/applicant-profile#ai-review).
  - [Reports](/hiring/reports) can now be filtered by job.
  - Company profiles show your company details and jobs you share, with an improved job view.
  - Jobs that require sign-in now ask candidates to sign in before applying. See [Enfue Authentication](/integrations/connected-tools#enfue-authentication).
  - Applicant sources are now recorded for applications from your company profile and CV submissions. See [Applicant sources](/settings/applications/applicant-source-management).
  - Improved search.
  - Improved personal workspaces: a better layout, a public view for people who aren't signed in, and Enfue remembers which workspace you chose.
</Update>

<Update label="Sep 27, 2026" description="Job seekers, bulk email, and evaluation options">
  - **New job seeker view**, so candidates can browse and apply for opportunities.
  - Candidates can apply for opportunities while getting started on Enfue.
  - **Bulk email:** send one email to many applicants at once from a new panel, with attachments. See [Bulk actions](/hiring/applicants/bulk-actions).
  - **Evaluation decision options:** rename the decision buttons and add a **Neutral** option. See [Custom fields for evaluation](/settings/applications/custom-fields-for-evaluation#settings).
  - When importing a candidate spreadsheet, you can invite candidates to join Enfue by email. See [Data migration](/settings/general/data-migration).
  - AI-generated job description sections now merge into a single description, with no length limits when you edit them. See [Create and edit a job](/hiring/jobs/create-a-job).
  - Improved the job details panel and the applicant panel.
  - New items in the Help menu, and an updated link for reporting issues. See [Get help](/help/overview).
  - Fixed talent pools not refreshing, and long custom evaluation fields overflowing the page.
</Update>

<Update label="Sep 20, 2026" description="Evaluation attachments and a smoother Inbox">
  - **Interviewers can attach files to evaluations**, and you can preview them on the applicant's evaluations. See [Evaluations](/hiring/evaluations).
  - **Append previous email** in the Inbox quotes the earlier message in your reply, and long threads take up less space. See [Inbox](/hiring/inbox#reply-to-an-applicant).
  - The email editor grows as you type.
  - Chat with an applicant directly from their panel, and open applicant details in full view.
  - New **APPLICANT FIRSTNAME** parameter for email templates. See [Email templates](/settings/applications/email-templates#parameters).
  - Disqualifying several applicants at once now opens in a side panel.
  - A refreshed stage header on the [Pipeline](/hiring/pipeline).
  - New onboarding for job seekers, with job search.
  - Sign-in and sign-up pages now support multiple languages, with a refreshed design.
  - Job detail pages work better on mobile.
  - Fixed talent pool descriptions not saving, and the interviewer list being hidden in some panels.
</Update>
