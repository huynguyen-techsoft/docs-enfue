<Update label="Apr 27, 2025" description="Smarter CSV import">
  - CSV import matches your columns to Enfue's fields, and you can view CV files. See [Data migration](/settings/general/data-migration).
  - Public and internal jobs are shown together, and "Interns" is now **Internship**.
  - A view of recent activities.
  - A company's industry and city are optional.
  - English and Vietnamese support across more pages.
  - Fixed being signed out after closing the browser, and messages on mobile.
</Update>

<Update label="Apr 20, 2025" description="English and Vietnamese">
  - **Enfue in English and Vietnamese,** including the landing page.
  - The applicant list shows when each applicant was last updated, and applicants can be marked as disqualified.
  - Keep your phone number and email private.
  - Longer job responsibilities, up to 2,000 characters, and improved evaluation notes.
  - Referral codes for subscriptions, and a new company sign-up form.
  - The **About** tab comes first on company profiles.
  - Clickable links in posts, and improved job recommendations.
</Update>

<Update label="Apr 13, 2025" description="Mobile version and job benefits">
  - **A mobile version of Enfue.**
  - An assistant on the landing page.
  - Job pages show benefits.
  - Create posts from your profile, and assign a company email.
  - District and street are optional in company addresses.
  - Delete confirmations, and fixes to messages, notifications, and profile photos.
</Update>

<Update label="Apr 6, 2025" description="Subscriptions and job viewers">
  - **Subscription plan limits** are applied.
  - See who viewed your published jobs.
  - A payment confirmation page.
  - Choose your company's username.
  - Edit posts.
  - A mobile landing page, and LinkedIn sync is always available.
  - Archived and internal jobs can't be viewed or saved by candidates.
  - Fixed video CVs and showing currency.
</Update>
