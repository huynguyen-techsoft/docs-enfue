<Update label="Mar 30, 2025" description="Payments, landing page, and duplicate jobs">
  - **Payments and invoices:** choose and view your subscription, and see your payments. See [Payments](/payments/overview).
  - **Duplicate a job.**
  - A new Enfue landing page, with Privacy and Terms pages.
  - "Open to work" moved to account settings, and you can change your profile link.
  - Many fixes to profiles, jobs, notifications, and sign-in.
</Update>

<Update label="Mar 23, 2025" description="Messaging and secure evaluation links">
  - **Messaging** between candidates and companies, with a **Message** button on company profiles. See [Messenger](/hiring/messenger).
  - **Secure evaluation links,** so interviewers can view an applicant and submit an evaluation without signing in. See [Evaluations](/hiring/evaluations).
  - **Manage company admins:** add, edit, and remove them. See [Admins](/settings/users/admins).
  - Subscription plans.
  - Search everything at once.
  - Share jobs and companies, and send invitations.
  - Accept or decline connection requests, and see recent and connected people.
  - Post detail pages, and an edit profile form.
</Update>

<Update label="Mar 16, 2025" description="Evaluation requests and LinkedIn sign-in">
  - **Ask interviewers to evaluate applicants.** See [Request an evaluation](/hiring/evaluations#request-an-evaluation).
  - Invite people to join Enfue.
  - Sign in with LinkedIn, and reset a forgotten password.
  - Video CVs.
  - Notifications, networking, and connection requests.
  - Share posts on social media, delete posts, and add certificates and skills to your profile.
  - A company dashboard.
</Update>

<Update label="Mar 9, 2025" description="Google sign-in and archived jobs">
  - **Sign up and sign in with Google.**
  - **Archive jobs,** and view your archived jobs.
  - Upload a video resume.
  - A company dashboard and networking list.
  - Save jobs, and see the jobs you applied for or saved.
  - Company admins can post as the company, and see their followers.
  - Preview a CV inside the applicant form, and a new font.
</Update>

<Update label="Mar 2, 2025" description="Applicant list and Excel import">
  - **The applicant list.** See [Applicants](/hiring/applicants/overview).
  - **Import applicants from an Excel file.**
  - Edit jobs as a company admin.
  - Send messages, search for people to message, and see new messages in real time.
  - Edit comments on posts.
</Update>
