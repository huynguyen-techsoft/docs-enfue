<Update label="Jul 26, 2026" description="A more powerful editor and evaluation form">
  - **Editor upgrades:** undo and redo, multi-column layouts, toggle blocks, a color palette, and a toolbar that adapts to your screen.
  - **Insert parameters into emails** with a new button, and a refreshed email templates screen. See [Email templates](/settings/applications/email-templates).
  - **Improved evaluation form:** scores are optional, you move to the next applicant automatically, and **Previous** and **Next** let you step through applicants. See [Evaluations](/hiring/evaluations).
  - More options when requesting evaluations for several applicants at once.
  - The **Apply** button on job pages is back.
  - More reliable [Messenger](/hiring/messenger), and fixes to the sidebar and applicant navigation.
</Update>

<Update label="Jul 19, 2026" description="New rich text editor and job lateness report">
  - **A new rich text editor** across Enfue, with better links, lists, code blocks, tables, and media.
  - **Job lateness report** in [Reports](/hiring/reports#open-job-lateness), and the job table can now be sorted.
  - [Talent pools](/hiring/talent-pools) can be sorted and show custom fields.
  - New colors for the match score.
  - Improved [custom evaluation fields](/settings/applications/custom-fields-for-evaluation).
  - A rebalanced [Interviews](/hiring/interviews) layout.
  - Tables remember your settings and default filters.
  - Notifications now cover applicants added without a job.
  - Fixed sending emails, and note previews now match the editor.
</Update>

<Update label="Jul 12, 2026" description="Match scores everywhere and team performance">
  - **Match scores in tables,** with a breakdown that opens by default and a hover preview. See [AI Review](/hiring/applicants/applicant-profile#ai-review).
  - **Team performance** and a job pipeline section in [Reports](/hiring/reports#team-performance-for-team-leaders).
  - **An improved job board,** with a board layout, **Report** and **Edit** tabs, and a sidebar. See [Jobs](/hiring/jobs/overview).
  - A refreshed email templates page.
  - Fixed the archived and active job filters, column names, and event pages.
</Update>

<Update label="Jul 5, 2026" description="New reports and interviews screens">
  - **A new Reports page** with an overview and recruiter activity. See [Reports](/hiring/reports).
  - **An improved Interviews screen.** See [Interviews](/hiring/interviews).
  - A new **Requester** column on the [Evaluations](/hiring/evaluations#the-evaluation-list) list.
  - Quick actions on [Tasks](/hiring/tasks).
  - A more consistent job board, and contact links work even without `https://`.
  - Enfue is faster, rebuilt on a new foundation.
  - Sharper logos and many bug fixes.
</Update>
