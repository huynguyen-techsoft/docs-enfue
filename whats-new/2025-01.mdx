<Update label="Jan 26, 2025" description="Evaluations and the Kanban board">
  - **Evaluations:** submit and edit evaluations of applicants. See [Evaluations](/hiring/evaluations).
  - **Move applicants between stages** on the Kanban board, and search it.
  - Upload an applicant's resume.
  - "Candidates" is now **Applicants**.
  - Edit and view your company profile, with cities and districts.
  - Fill in the applicant form from an Enfue profile.
  - Set your "open to work" status, with help from the AI assistant.
</Update>

<Update label="Jan 19, 2025" description="Applicant details and LinkedIn sync">
  - **Applicant details:** view and edit them.
  - **Sync from LinkedIn,** including into the add applicant form.
  - Your CV is saved to your profile when you apply.
  - Notes on applicant cards.
  - Fixed adding applicants without labels.
</Update>

<Update label="Jan 12, 2025" description="Application form">
  - Admins can apply for jobs on a candidate's behalf.
  - The form shows the job location, with improved formatting.
  - New applicants are added automatically.
</Update>

<Update label="Jan 5, 2025" description="App updates">
  - Candidates can apply for jobs.
  - Jobs appear on the Kanban board.
  - Fixed button spacing on smaller screens.
</Update>
