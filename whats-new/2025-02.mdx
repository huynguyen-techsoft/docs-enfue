<Update label="Feb 23, 2025" description="Filters and conversations">
  - **Filters** for applicant tracking, and a location filter on the Kanban board.
  - Start conversations and see your conversation list.
  - Comment on posts.
  - An improved Enfue assistant.
</Update>

<Update label="Feb 16, 2025" description="Posts and profiles">
  - Create posts.
  - Add your experience, education, and contact details to your personal profile.
  - Company profiles get a **Contact** tab and an **About** section, and admins can update contact details and locations.
  - Company admins can see their list of jobs.
  - Smoother scrolling on the Kanban board.
</Update>

<Update label="Feb 9, 2025" description="Stages and company profiles">
  - Applicant tracking now uses **stages** instead of statuses. See [Pipeline](/hiring/pipeline).
  - Clear an evaluation score you've selected.
  - **Edit your company profile:** about us, services, benefits, activities, photos, logo, cover image, and location. See [Career page](/workplace/career-page).
  - Edit your personal profile's about me, resume, and photos.
  - Comment on applicant details.
  - Salary on job details, English city names, and letter avatars.
  - Fixed uploading images over 3 MB.
</Update>
