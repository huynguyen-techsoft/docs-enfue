<Update label="Nov 30, 2025" description="Dashboard updates">
  - The dashboard shows applicants and interviews from the last seven days, and stages by assignee.
  - A share window appears after you create a job.
  - Fixed job list UI issues.
</Update>

<Update label="Nov 23, 2025" description="Minor changes">
  - Job management now uses tabs.
  - Choose a meeting type and add a phone number when signing up a company.
  - Fixed the company job list.
</Update>

<Update label="Nov 16, 2025" description="Features and fixes">
  - Filter reports by source.
  - Fixed dashboard and applicant editing UI issues.
</Update>

<Update label="Nov 9, 2025" description="Bulk editing in HRIS">
  - **Bulk edit** your people records in HRIS.
  - Checklists in the editor.
  - Choose a source from a dropdown.
  - Better spacing in the job list, internships, and notifications.
  - Fixed appointment and report page UI issues, plus other interface fixes.
</Update>

<Update label="Nov 2, 2025" description="New notifications and payment improvements">
  - **New notification types,** so you hear about more of what happens in your workspace. See [Notifications](/hiring/notifications).
  - **Payment improvements,** including all currency options for companies in Vietnam, and fixed values when changing plans. See [Subscription](/payments/subscription).
  - Easier, more reliable appointment scheduling.
  - Tables and line breaks in the editor.
  - Attach files when editing an applicant.
  - Fixed event and appointment details, and an error when opening a personal profile without signing in.
</Update>
