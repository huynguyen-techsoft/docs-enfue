<Update label="Jan 25, 2026" description="Features and fixes">
  - Fixed profile pictures not showing in notifications.
  - More filters for each job's [applicant list](/hiring/jobs/job-applicants).
  - Bulk emails now skip applicants without an email address.
</Update>

<Update label="Jan 18, 2026" description="New changes">
  - New [embedded job form](/hiring/jobs/job-integrations#embed-the-job-form-on-your-website) for your website.
  - New standalone application form.
  - Easier to [add applicants](/hiring/applicants/add-applicants) from LinkedIn and Enfue.
</Update>

<Update label="Jan 11, 2026" description="Reports update">
  - Reports are now available for each job. See [Job report](/hiring/jobs/job-report).
  - Improved and reorganized the [Reports](/hiring/reports) screen.
  - Fixed UI issues.
</Update>

<Update label="Jan 4, 2026" description="Evaluation improvements">
  - Improved the evaluations layout.
  - You can now edit evaluation history.
  - AI writes job descriptions for you. See [Create and edit a job](/hiring/jobs/create-a-job).
  - Evaluation statuses are now shown. See [Evaluation statuses](/hiring/evaluations#evaluation-statuses).
</Update>
