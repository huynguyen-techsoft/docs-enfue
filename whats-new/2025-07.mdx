<Update label="Jul 27, 2025" description="A new editor for posts">
  - A new text editor for posts.
  - A cleaner interface.
  - Fixed adding company admins, and editing posts and images.
</Update>

<Update label="Jul 20, 2025" description="Weekly dashboards and locations">
  - **Weekly applicant numbers** on the dashboard, with a yearly view and a weekly summary.
  - Reworked cities and locations, with an easier location picker.
  - Assigning a job to someone is now optional.
  - Improved applicant tracking and connections.
  - All of an applicant's appointments are shown.
  - A refreshed landing page with animations, and your personal profile can show your CV or skills first.
  - Sign-in takes you to the landing page.
</Update>

<Update label="Jul 13, 2025" description="Personal profiles and HR team details">
  - Jobs can include your HR team's information.
  - A redesigned personal profile page.
  - A refreshed, mobile-friendly landing page and updated sign-up screen.
  - Fixed invalid dates and "present" in experience levels.
</Update>

<Update label="Jul 6, 2025" description="Kanban reports and cover letters">
  - A report of how many applicants are in each column of your Kanban board. See [Pipeline](/hiring/pipeline).
  - The CV submission form includes a cover letter.
  - A new three-column explore page.
  - Portfolio links no longer need a specific format.
  - Fixes to appointments, quizzes, and creating talent pools.
</Update>
