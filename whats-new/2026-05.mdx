<Update label="May 31, 2026" description="Activity on Home and mobile improvements">
  - Recent activity now appears on [Home](/hiring/home).
  - Enfue works better on mobile.
  - Personal and company accounts now open on the right home page.
  - Scrolling fixes and small improvements.
</Update>

<Update label="May 24, 2026" description="Email template and evaluation improvements">
  - Rename your email templates. See [Email templates](/settings/applications/email-templates).
  - Pages are now organized into tabs.
  - A better layout for custom evaluation fields, plus evaluation fixes.
  - Jobs can use a general address instead of a specific one.
  - Updated sidebar items.
  - Fixed changing assignees and evaluation history.
</Update>

<Update label="May 17, 2026" description="Logs, rooms, and Slack">
  - **[Logs](/hiring/timeline):** a record of your team's activity on applicants.
  - **[Rooms](/settings/users/rooms):** manage meeting rooms and book them for interviews, with an updated interview form and calendar.
  - **Slack integration:** get Enfue notifications in Slack. See [Slack](/integrations/slack).
  - A dedicated page for each applicant.
  - More email templates, and a smoother experience.
  - See duplicate applicants, attach several files to an applicant, and CC people on emails.
</Update>

<Update label="May 10, 2026" description="Priority, ZIP uploads, and better evaluations">
  - Mark applicants as **high priority**.
  - Upload ZIP files, and request evaluations for several applicants at once.
  - Evaluation requests include the applicant's information, and external evaluation forms show your custom evaluation fields.
  - An **Action** column in the applicant list, and a consistent applicant table.
  - Improved custom fields.
  - Choose a specific job location, and add a detailed office address.
  - Improved moving cards between stages on the Kanban board, and revised filters.
  - The selected job and tab are kept in the page address.
  - Updated internship list and address display.
</Update>

<Update label="May 3, 2026" description="Changes and fixes">
  - Fixed email-related bugs.
  - Updated menus and the navigation bar.
  - Fixed redirects.
  - Fixed the behavior when choosing a job that's already selected.
</Update>
