<Update label="Sep 27, 2026" description="Job seekers, bulk email, and evaluation options">
  - **New job seeker view**, so candidates can browse and apply for opportunities.
  - Candidates can apply for opportunities while getting started on Enfue.
  - **Bulk email:** send one email to many applicants at once from a new panel, with attachments. See [Bulk actions](/hiring/applicants/bulk-actions).
  - **Evaluation decision options:** rename the decision buttons and add a **Neutral** option. See [Custom fields for evaluation](/settings/applications/custom-fields-for-evaluation#settings).
  - When importing a candidate spreadsheet, you can invite candidates to join Enfue by email. See [Data migration](/settings/general/data-migration).
  - AI-generated job description sections now merge into a single description, with no length limits when you edit them. See [Create and edit a job](/hiring/jobs/create-a-job).
  - Improved the job details panel and the applicant panel.
  - New items in the Help menu, and an updated link for reporting issues. See [Get help](/help/overview).
  - Fixed talent pools not refreshing, and long custom evaluation fields overflowing the page.
</Update>

<Update label="Sep 20, 2026" description="Evaluation attachments and a smoother Inbox">
  - **Interviewers can attach files to evaluations**, and you can preview them on the applicant's evaluations. See [Evaluations](/hiring/evaluations).
  - **Append previous email** in the Inbox quotes the earlier message in your reply, and long threads take up less space. See [Inbox](/hiring/inbox#reply-to-an-applicant).
  - The email editor grows as you type.
  - Chat with an applicant directly from their panel, and open applicant details in full view.
  - New **APPLICANT FIRSTNAME** parameter for email templates. See [Email templates](/settings/applications/email-templates#parameters).
  - Disqualifying several applicants at once now opens in a side panel.
  - A refreshed stage header on the [Pipeline](/hiring/pipeline).
  - New onboarding for job seekers, with job search.
  - Sign-in and sign-up pages now support multiple languages, with a refreshed design.
  - Job detail pages work better on mobile.
  - Fixed talent pool descriptions not saving, and the interviewer list being hidden in some panels.
</Update>

<Update label="Sep 13, 2026" description="Enfue on mobile, and a clearer Inbox">
  - **Enfue works on your phone:** Home, Inbox, Messenger, applicants, the application form, evaluations, and the job list now adapt to small screens, with mobile navigation and workspace switching. You can read and reply to emails on mobile. See [On Mobile](/mobile/overview).
  - The [Inbox](/hiring/inbox) now shows email addresses and exact sent and received times, and displays attachments.
  - The interviewer's evaluation page now shows the applicant's documents and AI match score. See [Evaluations](/hiring/evaluations#completing-an-evaluation-for-interviewers).
  - [Home](/hiring/home) shows interviews with the job title and the stage at the time of scheduling, plus a report badge.
  - Choose whether each custom field appears on the application form, with a new **Show in form for applicant** column. See [Custom fields for applicants](/settings/applications/custom-fields-for-applicants).
  - Application form settings are now set per company. See [Application form](/settings/applications/application-form).
  - Customize the labels on your public job pages. See [Language labels](/settings/applications/application-form#language-labels).
  - Edit an applicant's LinkedIn URL from their overview.
  - The application form editor has the full formatting toolbar.
  - A cleaner layout for applicant details at the top of emails.
  - Fixed the CC list in the Calendar and loading more talent pools when disqualifying.
</Update>

<Update label="Sep 6, 2026" description="Faster scheduling and evaluation">
  - **Streamlined evaluating and scheduling interviews**, which now open in side panels instead of pop-up windows.
  - The [Pipeline](/hiring/pipeline) uses your own stage names and keeps applicants in a stable order.
  - Search applicants by email address and phone number, as well as name.
  - [Reports](/hiring/reports) now use your company's stage names, and exporting as PDF handles long reports, with a loading indicator.
  - Each applicant's emails open on their own page.
  - A polished applicant header, with clearer badges, a source tooltip, and panel actions.
  - A simpler evaluation list.
  - Fixed online-meeting details on in-person interviews, flickering while editing applicant details, and the Inbox.
</Update>
