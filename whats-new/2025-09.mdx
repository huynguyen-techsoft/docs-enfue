<Update label="Sep 28, 2025" description="Enhancements">
  - CSV imports now include more fields: note, salary, and title.
  - Applicants can have a birth date.
  - Add applicants to a talent pool while importing.
</Update>

<Update label="Sep 21, 2025" description="Email enhancements">
  - Compose a new email from an applicant's profile.
  - New [email templates](/settings/applications/email-templates).
  - Fixed a layout bug.
</Update>

<Update label="Sep 14, 2025" description="Bulk actions for applicants">
  - Take actions on many applicants at once. See [Bulk actions](/hiring/applicants/bulk-actions).
  - Export applicants as CSV.
  - Update the stage of many applicants at once.
</Update>

<Update label="Sep 7, 2025" description="Improved editor">
  - A smarter editor with more options.
  - The new editor is used for posts, jobs, and internships.
</Update>
