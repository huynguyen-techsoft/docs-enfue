<Update label="Jun 28, 2026" description="Applicant source management and a new job dashboard">
  - **Applicant source management:** manage the list of sources your team can choose from. See [Applicant sources](/settings/applications/applicant-source-management).
  - **A new job applicant dashboard,** with a full-screen view.
  - Source logos everywhere sources are shown.
  - **Previous** and **Next** buttons in the applicant panel.
  - Edit and delete comments.
  - The applicant's details now appear next to conversations in the [Inbox](/hiring/inbox#act-on-the-applicant-without-leaving-the-inbox).
  - A **Source** column and sorting on [Tasks](/hiring/tasks).
  - Interview locations are suggested from your company's offices, and labels match your connected meeting tool.
  - A refreshed sidebar and header, and notes get a default title based on their date.
  - An improved evaluation screen, and many fixes.
</Update>

<Update label="Jun 21, 2026" description="Tasks, evaluations, and a new Notes page">
  - **New: [Tasks](/hiring/tasks),** your working list of applicants who need a decision.
  - **New: evaluations** you can create for applicants, with an updated layout. See [Evaluations](/hiring/evaluations).
  - **A new [Notes](/hiring/notes) page.**
  - **A Duplicates tab** next to each job's applicants. See [Duplicates](/hiring/jobs/duplicates).
  - [Logs](/hiring/timeline) use your own stage names and record who added each applicant.
  - Recent activities on [Home](/hiring/home) show the job title.
  - Attachments can now be up to 5 MB.
  - Email templates need a subject, plus email template fixes.
  - Home and Reports improvements.
</Update>

<Update label="Jun 14, 2026" description="AI job descriptions and a new Home page">
  - **Create JD for me:** let AI write your job description. See [Create and edit a job](/hiring/jobs/create-a-job).
  - **An improved [Home](/hiring/home) page.**
  - New sign-in and sign-up pages, in English and Vietnamese.
  - Sort the applicant table by any column, and export exactly the columns you see, including custom fields, to CSV.
  - An **Add applicants** button in the header.
  - An extra **Save** button when editing a job, and optional industry and experience fields.
  - Set a default interview length.
  - A cleaner layout without an extra scrollbar.
  - Fixed tags showing codes instead of names, the **View Kanban** button, and the duplicates table.
</Update>

<Update label="Jun 7, 2026" description="Longer job descriptions">
  - Longer job descriptions, responsibilities, and qualifications.
  - Fixes to the Kanban board layout and filters, the job report tab, and the company home page.
</Update>
